### Interview Questions

**Q1. What are prerequisites in a security procedure?**  
**Answer:** Prerequisites are the requirements that must be met before performing the procedure.

**Q2. Why are prerequisites important?**  
**Answer:** They ensure that the person performing the procedure has everything required to complete it safely and correctly.

**Q3. What can be included as a prerequisite?**  
**Answer:** Required software, hardware, permissions, accounts, tools, or access.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. You create a procedure for changing Windows security settings, but the user does not have administrator privileges. What should you mention before the procedure?**

**Answer:** Administrator access should be listed as a prerequisite.
