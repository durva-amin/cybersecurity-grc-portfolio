### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a flowchart?**  
**Answer:** A flowchart visually represents the steps and sequence of a process.

**Q2. Why are flowcharts useful in security documentation?**  
**Answer:** They make procedures and decision-making processes easier to understand and follow.

**Q3. What do arrows represent in a flowchart?**  
**Answer:** Arrows show the direction or sequence of the process.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. A company wants to document the steps employees should follow when they receive a phishing email. What visual format could be used?**

**Answer:** A flowchart can show the sequence, such as **Receive email → Identify suspicious content → Report → Delete**.
