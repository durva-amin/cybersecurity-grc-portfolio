### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a caption in documentation?**  
**Answer:** A caption is a short description that explains the purpose or content of a screenshot, figure, or diagram.

**Q2. Why are captions important?**  
**Answer:** They help readers quickly understand what the visual evidence represents.

**Q3. Where is a caption usually placed?**  
**Answer:** Usually below the screenshot, image, or figure.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. You have five screenshots in a security report. How can you help the reader identify what each screenshot represents?**

**Answer:** Add a clear and descriptive caption to each screenshot.
