### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is impact in a security finding?**  
**Answer:** Impact describes what could happen or what could be affected if a security issue is not addressed.

**Q2. Why is impact important?**  
**Answer:** It helps an organization understand the potential consequences of a security issue and prioritize appropriate action.

**Q3. What can be affected by a security issue?**  
**Answer:** Systems, data, users, business operations, or the organization's security and compliance.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. You find that sensitive customer data is accessible to unauthorized users. What should you document as the impact?**

**Answer:** Unauthorized access could lead to data exposure, privacy issues, and potential compliance consequences.
