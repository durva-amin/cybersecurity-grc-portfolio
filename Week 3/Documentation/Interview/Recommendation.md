### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a recommendation in a security finding?**  
**Answer:** It is a suggested action to address an identified security issue or reduce its associated risk.

**Q2. What makes a good security recommendation?**  
**Answer:** It should be clear, practical, relevant to the identified issue, and help reduce the associated risk.

**Q3. What is the difference between impact and recommendation?**  
**Answer:** Impact explains **what could happen**, while recommendation explains **what should be done** to address the issue.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. You find that employees are using weak passwords. What recommendation could you provide?**

**Answer:** Implement a strong password policy and educate employees about creating and protecting strong passwords.
