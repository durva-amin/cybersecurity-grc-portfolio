### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is an evidence table?**  
**Answer:** It is a structured table used to document security observations and the evidence, impact, and recommendations related to them.

**Q2. Why are evidence tables useful?**  
**Answer:** They organize security findings clearly and make them easier to review and track.

**Q3. What information can an evidence table contain?**  
**Answer:** Observation, evidence, impact, and recommendation.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. You identify five security issues during a review. How can you present them clearly to management?**

**Answer:** Use an evidence table to organize each observation along with its supporting evidence, impact, and recommendation.
