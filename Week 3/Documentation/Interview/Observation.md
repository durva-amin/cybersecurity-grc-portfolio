### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a security observation?**  
**Answer:** A security observation is something identified during a review that may indicate a weakness, issue, or area requiring attention.

**Q2. Is every observation automatically a security risk?**  
**Answer:** No. An observation may require further analysis to determine whether it creates an actual risk.

**Q3. What should an observation be based on?**  
**Answer:** It should be based on facts, evidence, or something actually observed during the review.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. During a security review, you find that several employees have not enabled screen locking on their computers. What would you document first?**

**Answer:** Document the finding as an **observation**, supported by appropriate evidence.
