### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a technical diagram?**  
**Answer:** A technical diagram visually represents technical components and how they are connected or related.

**Q2. Why are technical diagrams useful in security documentation?**  
**Answer:** They make complex technical environments easier to understand and help identify components, connections, and security controls.

**Q3. What should a good technical diagram contain?**  
**Answer:** Relevant components, clear connections, meaningful labels, and a simple structure.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. A company wants to document how traffic flows from the Internet to its internal servers. What would you use?**

**Answer:** A network diagram showing the Internet, firewall, router, switches, and servers with clear connections.
