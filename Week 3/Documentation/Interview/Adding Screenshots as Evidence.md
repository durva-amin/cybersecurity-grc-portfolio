### Interview Questions

**Q1. Why are screenshots added to security documentation?**  
**Answer:** To provide visual evidence supporting a configuration, observation, or security finding.

**Q2. What should a screenshot contain?**  
**Answer:** It should clearly show the relevant information and enough context to understand the evidence.

**Q3. What should be done before adding a screenshot?**  
**Answer:** Remove unnecessary information, crop or highlight the relevant area, and add a clear caption.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. You find that a Linux firewall is enabled and need to document it for an audit. What can you include?**

**Answer:** A clear screenshot showing the firewall status, along with a descriptive caption identifying what the screenshot demonstrates.
