### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is the purpose of taking screenshots in security documentation?**  
**Answer:** Screenshots provide visual evidence that a configuration, setting, or activity was observed.

**Q2. What makes a screenshot useful as evidence?**  
**Answer:** It should be clear, relevant, readable, and show enough context to support the documented observation.

**Q3. Should you capture the entire screen every time?**  
**Answer:** No. Capture only the relevant information while keeping enough context to understand it.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. You are documenting Windows Defender settings. You take a screenshot, but the important security setting is too small to read. Is this good evidence?**

**Answer:** No. The screenshot should be retaken or appropriately cropped so the relevant security setting is clearly visible.
