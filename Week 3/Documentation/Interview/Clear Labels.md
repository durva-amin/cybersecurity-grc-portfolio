### Interview Questions

**Q1. Why are clear labels important in technical diagrams?**  
**Answer:** They help readers quickly understand what each component or connection represents.

**Q2. What makes a good label?**  
**Answer:** It should be short, specific, understandable, and relevant to the component.

**Q3. What is an example of a poor label?**  
**Answer:** Labels such as "Device 1" or "System A" when the actual component can be identified more clearly.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. A network diagram contains five boxes labeled Device 1, Device 2, Device 3, etc. How would you improve it?**

**Answer:** Replace the unclear labels with descriptive names such as **Firewall, Router, Switch, Server, and Endpoint**.
