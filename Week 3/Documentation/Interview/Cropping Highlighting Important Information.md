### Interview Questions

**Q1. Why should screenshots be cropped?**  
**Answer:** To remove unnecessary information and make the relevant evidence easier to understand.

**Q2. Why is highlighting useful?**  
**Answer:** It helps the reader quickly identify the important setting, value, or observation.

**Q3. Should you modify the actual information in a screenshot?**  
**Answer:** No. Highlighting should identify information, not alter or misrepresent the evidence.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. You have a screenshot containing 20 settings, but only one setting is relevant to your security finding. What should you do?**

**Answer:** Crop the unnecessary areas and highlight the relevant setting while keeping enough context to understand the evidence.
