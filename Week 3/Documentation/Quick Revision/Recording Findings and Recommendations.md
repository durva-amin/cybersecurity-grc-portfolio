### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Finding = What is the issue?**  
**Evidence = What proves it?**  
**Impact = What could happen?**  
**Recommendation = What should be done?**

### Quick Revision

- Record security issues clearly.
- Include **Observation/ Finding + Evidence + Impact + Recommendation**.
- Recommendations should address the identified issue.
- Proper documentation helps with **tracking and follow-up**.
