### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Observation → What did you find?**  
**Evidence → What proves it?**  
**Impact → What could happen?**  
**Recommendation → What should be done?**

### Quick Revision

- Recommendation = **Suggested action to address a security issue**.
- Should be practical and relevant.
- It should help **reduce risk**.
- Think: **"What should we do about it?"**
