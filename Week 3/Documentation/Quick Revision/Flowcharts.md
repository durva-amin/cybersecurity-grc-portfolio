### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Flowchart = Process + Sequence + Decision/Steps**

Use a flowchart when you need to explain **how something happens step by step**.

### Quick Revision

- Flowchart = Visual representation of a process.
- Shows **sequence and decisions**.
- Arrows show the **flow**.
- Useful for security procedures and incident processes.
- Keep it simple and easy to follow.
