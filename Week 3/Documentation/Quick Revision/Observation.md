### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Observation = What did you notice?**

Don't immediately assume the impact. First document **what was actually observed**, then analyze the risk.

### Quick Revision

- Observation = **Something identified during a review**.
- Should be **fact-based**.
- Support it with **evidence**.
- An observation may lead to a **risk/finding** after analysis.
- Think: **"What did I see?"**
