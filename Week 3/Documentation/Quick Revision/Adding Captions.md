### Interview Tip ⭐

A good caption should answer:

**"What does this screenshot show?"**

Keep it **short, specific, and descriptive**.

### Quick Revision

**Caption = Short description of visual evidence.**

- Explain what the screenshot shows.
- Keep it concise.
- Use consistent numbering such as **Figure 1, Figure 2, Figure 3**.
- Place the caption near the relevant screenshot.
