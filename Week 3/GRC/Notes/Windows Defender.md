## Definition

**Windows Defender**, now commonly referred to as **Microsoft Defender Antivirus**, is Microsoft's built-in antivirus and antimalware protection for Windows.

**Simple Definition:**

> Windows Defender helps **detect, prevent, and remove malware** from a Windows computer.

---

## What Does Windows Defender Do?

It helps protect against:

- Viruses
- Trojans
- Worms
- Ransomware
- Spyware
- Other malware

It can:

- Scan files and applications.
- Monitor the system for suspicious activity.
- Detect known and potentially unwanted threats.
- Quarantine or remove detected threats.
- Provide real-time protection.


## Types of Scans

Microsoft Defender Antivirus can perform different types of scans, including:

### Quick Scan

Checks common locations where malware may be found.

### Full Scan

Scans the entire system.

### Custom Scan

Scans specific files or folders.

### Offline Scan

Restarts the computer and scans before normal Windows operation, which can help detect persistent threats.
