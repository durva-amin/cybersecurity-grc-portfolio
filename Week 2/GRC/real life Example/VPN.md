An employee is working from home and needs to access the company's internal systems.

They connect to the company's VPN.

**Laptop → VPN → Company Network**

The VPN provides a secure connection for remote access.


## VPN and Public Wi-Fi

Suppose you are using Wi-Fi at a café.

A VPN can encrypt traffic between your device and the VPN server, providing additional protection against certain network-level eavesdropping risks on the local network.

However, a VPN **does not make you completely anonymous or protect against every cyber threat**.
