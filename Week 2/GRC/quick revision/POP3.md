- **POP3 = Post Office Protocol version 3**
- Used for **receiving/retrieving emails**
- Mainly **downloads emails** to the device.
- **Port 110 → POP3**
- **Port 995 → Secure POP3**
- **SMTP → Send**
- **POP3 → Download/Retrieve**
