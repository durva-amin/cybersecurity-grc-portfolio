- **IMAP = Internet Message Access Protocol**
- Used to **access and manage emails**.
- Emails are primarily stored on the **mail server**.
- Good for **multiple devices**.
- **Port 143 → Standard IMAP**
- **Port 993 → IMAP over TLS**
- **SMTP → Send**
- **POP3 → Download**
- **IMAP → Synchronize**
