- **IPv4 = Internet Protocol version 4**
- **32-bit** address
- **4 octets**
- Each octet = **8 bits**
- Each octet ranges from **0–255**
- Example: **192.168.1.10**
- Limited address space led to the development of **IPv6**



### Easy Trick to Remember

**IPv4 = 4 parts**

`192 . 168 . 1 . 10`

**4 octets × 8 bits = 32 bits**
