- **IDS = Intrusion Detection System**
- **Detects** suspicious activity.
- Generates **alerts**.
- **NIDS** → Network monitoring.
- **HIDS** → Host/system monitoring.
- Primarily **detects and alerts**, rather than automatically blocking.


### Easy Trick

**IDS → Detects 🚨**

**IPS → Prevents/Blocks 🛑**
