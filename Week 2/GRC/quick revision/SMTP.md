- **SMTP = Simple Mail Transfer Protocol**
- Mainly used for **sending emails**.
- Common ports:
    - **25**
    - **587**
    - **465**
- **SMTP → Send**
- **POP3 → Retrieve/download**
- **IMAP → Access & synchronize**

**SMTP → Sending 📤**
