- **VPN = Virtual Private Network**
- Creates a **secure connection/tunnel**.
- Encrypts traffic between the device and VPN server.
- Useful for **remote access**.
- Helpful on **untrusted networks**.
- Does **not** provide complete anonymity.




### Easy Trick

**VPN = Secure Tunnel 🔒**

> **Device → Encrypted Tunnel → VPN Server → Destination**
