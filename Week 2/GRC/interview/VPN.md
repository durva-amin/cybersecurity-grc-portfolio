# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a VPN?

**Answer:**  
A VPN is a technology that creates a secure, encrypted connection between a device and a VPN server over a network such as the Internet.

### Q2. What does VPN stand for?

**Answer:**  
**Virtual Private Network.**

### Q3. Why is a VPN used?

**Answer:**  
It is used to protect network traffic and provide secure remote access to private networks.

### Q4. Does a VPN provide complete anonymity?

**Answer:**  
No. A VPN can provide privacy and encrypt traffic between the device and VPN server, but it does not make the user completely anonymous.

### Q5. Can companies use VPNs for remote employees?

**Answer:**  
Yes. Organizations commonly use VPNs to allow employees to securely access internal resources from outside the organization's network.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. An employee is working from home and needs secure access to the company's internal network. Which technology can be used?

**Answer:**  
A **VPN**, because it can create a secure connection between the employee's device and the company's private network.
