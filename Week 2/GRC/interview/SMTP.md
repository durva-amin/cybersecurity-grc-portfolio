# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is SMTP?

**Answer:**  
SMTP is a network protocol used to send email messages from clients to mail servers and between mail servers.

### Q2. What does SMTP stand for?

**Answer:**  
**Simple Mail Transfer Protocol.**

### Q3. Is SMTP used for sending or receiving emails?

**Answer:**  
SMTP is mainly used for **sending email**.

### Q4. What are common SMTP ports?

**Answer:**  
**25, 587, and 465** are commonly associated with SMTP.

### Q5. What is the difference between SMTP and IMAP?

**Answer:**  
**SMTP is mainly used for sending email**, while **IMAP is used to access and synchronize emails stored on a mail server.**

### Q6. What is the difference between SMTP and POP3?

**Answer:**  
SMTP is mainly used for **sending email**, while POP3 is used for **retrieving email**, typically by downloading messages from the server.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. An employee can receive emails but cannot send any emails. Which protocol should be checked first for outgoing mail?

**Answer:**  
**SMTP**, because SMTP handles outgoing email submission and delivery.
