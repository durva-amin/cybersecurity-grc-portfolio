## Definition

A **Hub** is a basic networking device that **connects multiple devices in a LAN and broadcasts incoming data to all connected ports.**

**Simple Definition:**

> A Hub sends incoming data to **every connected device**, instead of sending it only to the intended device.

---

## How Does a Hub Work?

1. A device sends data to the hub.
2. The hub receives the data.
3. The hub copies/broadcasts the signal to **all other ports**.
4. Each connected device receives the data, but only the intended device processes it.

**Simple flow:**

**PC 1 → Hub → PC 2, PC 3, PC 4**

---

## Characteristics of a Hub

- Does not use MAC addresses to make forwarding decisions.
- Sends traffic to all connected ports.
- Creates one shared collision domain.
- Usually operates at **Layer 1 (Physical Layer)**.
- Less efficient than a switch.
- Modern networks generally use switches instead of hubs.
