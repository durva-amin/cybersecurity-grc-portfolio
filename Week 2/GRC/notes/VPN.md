## Definition

**VPN (Virtual Private Network)** is a technology that creates a **secure, encrypted connection between a device and a VPN server over a network such as the Internet.**

**Simple Definition:**

> A VPN creates a **secure tunnel** for network communication.

---

## What Does a VPN Do?

A VPN can:

- Encrypt network traffic between the device and VPN server.
- Help protect data when using untrusted networks such as public Wi-Fi.
- Allow users to securely access a private organization network remotely.
- Hide the user's original IP address from websites by making the VPN server's IP appear as the source for Internet traffic.

---

## How Does a VPN Work?

1. Your device connects to a VPN server.
2. A secure connection is established.
3. Your traffic travels through the encrypted VPN connection.
4. The VPN server forwards the traffic to its destination.

**Simple flow:**

**Your Device → 🔒 VPN Tunnel → VPN Server → Internet**
