## Definition

A **Switch** is a networking device that **connects multiple devices within the same local network (LAN) and forwards data to the correct device using MAC addresses.**

**Simple Definition:**

> A Switch connects devices in a local network and sends data **only to the intended device**.

---

## What Does a Switch Do?

- Connects computers, printers, servers, and other devices in a LAN.
- Uses **MAC addresses** to forward Ethernet frames.
- Learns which MAC address is connected to which port.
- Reduces unnecessary network traffic compared with a traditional hub.

---

## How Does a Switch Work?

1. A device sends an Ethernet frame.
2. The switch checks the **destination MAC address**.
3. It looks at its **MAC address table**.
4. It forwards the frame through the appropriate port.

**Simple flow:**

**PC → Switch → Destination PC**
