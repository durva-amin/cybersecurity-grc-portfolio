## Definition

A **Proxy Server** is a server that **acts as an intermediary between a client and the destination server**.

**Simple Definition:**

> A proxy sits **between you and the Internet** and forwards requests on your behalf.

---

## How Does a Proxy Work?

Without a proxy:

**Your Device → Website**

With a proxy:

**Your Device → Proxy Server → Website**

The proxy receives your request, forwards it to the destination, and sends the response back to you.

---

## What Can a Proxy Server Do?

Depending on its configuration, a proxy can:

- Filter web traffic.
- Control access to websites.
- Log network activity.
- Cache frequently requested content.
- Hide the client's IP address from the destination server.


## Proxy vs VPN

|Proxy Server|VPN|
|---|---|
|Acts as an intermediary for configured traffic|Creates a secure connection/tunnel|
|Often used for specific applications or traffic|Can protect traffic from the device through the VPN connection|
|May provide filtering and caching|Commonly used for secure remote access|
|Encryption is not inherent to all proxies|VPN connections commonly use encryption|
