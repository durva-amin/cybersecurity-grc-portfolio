## Definition

A **Table** is a structured arrangement of information in **rows and columns** that makes data easier to read, compare, and organize.

### Simple Definition

**Table = Organizing information into rows and columns.**

---

## Why are Tables Important?

- Organize information clearly.
- Make comparisons easier.
- Improve readability.
- Present large amounts of information in a compact format.
- Make technical and security documentation more professional.

---

## Common Uses in GRC Documentation

Tables can be used for:

- Asset information
- Risk registers
- Security controls
- Network devices
- IP addresses
- Protocols and ports
- Version history
- Compliance requirements
