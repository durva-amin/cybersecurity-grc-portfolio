## Definition

**Header** is the area at the top of each page, while a **Footer** is the area at the bottom of each page. They are used to display repeated document information.

### Simple Definition

**Header = Information at the top of the page.**  
**Footer = Information at the bottom of the page.**

---

## Why are Headers & Footers Important?

- Make documents look professional.
- Identify the document on every page.
- Provide important document information.
- Help with document navigation and management.
- Can contain page numbers, document title, version, or classification.

---

## Common Information in Headers

- Document title
- Organization name
- Document ID
- Document classification

**Example:**

> Information Security Terms Glossary | Version 1.0

---

## Common Information in Footers

- Page number
- Version
- Confidentiality classification
- Copyright information

**Example:**

> Page 2 of 5
