## Definition

**Technical Documentation** explains technical information in a way that is **clear, accurate, and easy for the intended reader to understand**.

### Simple Definition

**Explain technical concepts simply without losing the important technical meaning.**

---

## Why is Clear Explanation Important?

- Makes technical information easier to understand.
- Helps non-technical people understand security concepts.
- Reduces confusion and misunderstandings.
- Helps during audits and reviews.
- Makes documentation useful for different teams.

---

## How to Explain a Technical Concept

When explaining something technical:

1. **Start with the basic meaning.**
2. **Explain how it works.**
3. **Give a simple example.**
4. Use **clear and consistent terminology**.
5. Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.

### Example: Firewall

**Definition:**  
A firewall is a security control that monitors and controls network traffic.

**Explanation:**  
It allows or blocks traffic based on defined security rules.

**Example:**  
A firewall may block unauthorized incoming connections to an organization's network.
