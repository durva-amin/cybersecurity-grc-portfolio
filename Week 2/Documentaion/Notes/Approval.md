## Definition

**Approval** is the formal confirmation that a document has been reviewed and accepted by the authorized person.

### Simple Definition

**Approval = Official permission to use the document.**

---

## Why is Approval Important?

- Confirms the document has been reviewed.
- Ensures the document is accepted by the appropriate authority.
- Prevents unauthorized documents from being used.
- Supports document control and compliance.
- Provides evidence during audits.

---

## What Can Approval Include?

A document may record:

- **Approver Name**
- **Approver Role/Designation**
- **Approval Date**
- **Approval Status**

Example:

> **Approved By:** Information Security Manager  
> **Approval Date:** 10 August 2026  
> **Status:** Approved
