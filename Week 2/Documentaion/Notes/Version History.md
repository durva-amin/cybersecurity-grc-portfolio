## Definition

**Version History** is a record of the different versions of a document and the changes made in each version.

### Simple Definition

**Version History = Record of what changed in the document over time.**

---

## Why is Version History Important?

- Tracks document changes.
- Shows who made the changes.
- Helps identify previous versions.
- Prevents confusion between document versions.
- Provides evidence during audits.
