## Definition

**Risk** is the possibility that a threat will exploit a vulnerability and cause harm to an organization.

**Observation** is a finding or condition noticed during a review, assessment, or audit that may require attention.

### Simple Definition

**Risk = What could go wrong and how it could affect the organization.**

**Observation = What you noticed during an assessment.**

---

## Why are Risks and Observations Important?

- Help identify security weaknesses.
- Support risk assessment.
- Help organizations prioritize improvements.
- Provide useful information during audits.
- Help management understand security issues.

---

## Example

### Observation

> The company's network documentation does not include IP addresses for several servers.

### Risk

> Incomplete network information may make it difficult to identify and manage network assets, increasing the risk of unauthorized or unmanaged devices remaining unnoticed.

The **observation describes what was found**, while the **risk explains the possible impact**.
