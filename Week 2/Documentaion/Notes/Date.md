## Definition

**Date** indicates when a document was created, issued, updated, or approved.

### Simple Definition

**Date = When something happened to the document.**

---

## Why is Date Important?

- Shows when the document was created or updated.
- Helps track document history.
- Supports version control.
- Helps determine whether information is current.
- Provides useful evidence during audits.

---

## Common Types of Dates

A document may have different dates, such as:

- **Creation Date** → When the document was created.
- **Issue Date** → When the document was officially released.
- **Updated Date** → When the document was last changed.
- **Approval Date** → When the document was approved.
