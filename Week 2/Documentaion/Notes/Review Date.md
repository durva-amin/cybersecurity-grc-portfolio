## Definition

A **Review Date** is the date when a document is scheduled to be checked to ensure that its information is still accurate, relevant, and up to date.

### Simple Definition

**Review Date = When the document should be checked again.**

---

## Why is Review Date Important?

- Ensures documents are reviewed regularly.
- Helps identify outdated information.
- Supports compliance requirements.
- Helps maintain accurate security documentation.
- Provides evidence of document management during audits.
