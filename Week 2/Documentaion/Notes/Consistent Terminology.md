## Definition

**Consistent Terminology** means using the **same terms and wording consistently** throughout a document when referring to the same concept.

### Simple Definition

**Use the same term for the same thing throughout the document.**

---

## Why is Consistent Terminology Important?

- Prevents confusion.
- Makes technical documentation easier to understand.
- Improves professionalism.
- Reduces ambiguity.
- Helps different teams understand the same information consistently.

---

## Example

Suppose your document refers to a device as:

- "Router" in one section
- "Network Router" in another
- "Gateway Device" in another

If all three refer to the same device, this can create confusion.

Choose one appropriate term, such as **Router**, and use it consistently.
