## Definition

**Page Numbers** are numbers added to document pages to show their order and make it easier to navigate through the document.

### Simple Definition

**Page Numbers = Identifying the order of pages.**

---

## Why are Page Numbers Important?

- Make documents easier to navigate.
- Help readers find specific pages.
- Make printed documents easier to organize.
- Help auditors reference specific sections or pages.
- Make technical documents more professional.

---

## Common Page Number Formats

Examples:

- **1, 2, 3, 4**
- **Page 1**
- **Page 1 of 10**

For your Week 2 project, **Page 1 of X** is a professional option.
