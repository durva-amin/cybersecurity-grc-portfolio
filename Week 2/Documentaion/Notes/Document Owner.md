## Definition

A **Document Owner** is the person responsible for maintaining, reviewing, and ensuring the accuracy of a document.

### Simple Definition

**Document Owner = The person responsible for the document.**

---

## Why is a Document Owner Important?

- Provides clear responsibility.
- Ensures the document stays accurate and up to date.
- Coordinates reviews and updates.
- Helps during audits.
- Ensures the document follows organizational requirements.

---

## What Does a Document Owner Do?

A Document Owner may be responsible for:

- Maintaining the document.
- Reviewing it periodically.
- Coordinating updates.
- Ensuring required approvals are obtained.
- Making sure the latest approved version is available.
