Suppose your Network Basics & Security Documentation has 10 pages.

The footer can show:

> **Page 4 of 10**

An auditor can immediately identify where page 4 belongs and reference it easily.



## How to Add Page Numbers in Word

1. Go to **Insert**.
2. Select **Page Number**.
3. Choose **Bottom of Page**.
4. Select a simple format.
5. Word automatically numbers the pages.
