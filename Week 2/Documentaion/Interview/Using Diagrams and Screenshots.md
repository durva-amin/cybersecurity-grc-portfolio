## Interview Questions

### Q1. Why are diagrams used in technical documentation?

**Answer:**  
Diagrams visually represent systems, connections, or processes and make complex technical information easier to understand.

### Q2. When would you use a screenshot?

**Answer:**  
When documenting software settings, configurations, procedures, or steps that are easier to understand visually.

### Q3. What is the difference between a diagram and a screenshot?

**Answer:**  
A diagram is created to represent a system, process, or relationship visually, while a screenshot captures an actual screen or interface.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** You need to explain how devices in a company's network connect to the internet. Would you use a paragraph or a network diagram?

**Answer:**  
A **network diagram**, because it can clearly show the relationships and connections between the network components.
