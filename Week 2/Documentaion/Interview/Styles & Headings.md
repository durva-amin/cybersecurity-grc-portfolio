## Interview Questions

### Q1. What are Styles in Microsoft Word?

**Answer:**  
Styles are predefined formatting settings used to apply consistent formatting to text throughout a document.

### Q2. What are Headings?

**Answer:**  
Headings are styles used to organize a document into sections and subsections.

### Q3. Why are Heading Styles important in technical documentation?

**Answer:**  
They create a clear structure, improve readability, make navigation easier, and allow automatic generation of a Table of Contents.

### Q4. What is the difference between Heading 1 and Heading 2?

**Answer:**  
Heading 1 is used for major sections, while Heading 2 is used for subsections within those sections.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** You have a 15-page network security document. The headings were manually formatted using bold and larger font. Later, you need to create a Table of Contents. What should you have used?

**Answer:**  
**Heading Styles**, because Word can automatically create a Table of Contents from properly applied heading styles.
