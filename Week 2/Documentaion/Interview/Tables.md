## Interview Questions

### Q1. Why are tables used in technical documentation?

**Answer:**  
Tables organize information clearly and make data easier to read, compare, and understand.

### Q2. Where can tables be used in GRC?

**Answer:**  
They can be used for risk registers, asset inventories, security controls, compliance requirements, and version history.

### Q3. What is the advantage of using a table instead of a paragraph?

**Answer:**  
A table presents structured information more clearly and makes comparison easier.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** You need to document 20 network devices along with their names, IP addresses, and device types. Would you use a paragraph or a table?

**Answer:**  
A **table**, because the information has consistent fields and needs to be easily compared.
