## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is document approval?

**Answer:**  
Document approval is the formal confirmation that an authorized person has reviewed and accepted a document.

### Q2. Why is approval important in GRC?

**Answer:**  
It ensures that documents are formally reviewed and authorized before they are implemented or used.

### Q3. Who can approve a document?

**Answer:**  
An authorized person, such as a manager, department head, or designated document approver, depending on the organization's approval process.

### Q4. Is the Author always the Approver?

**Answer:**  
No. The author creates the document, while the approver provides formal authorization.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** An employee creates a new security policy and immediately distributes it to everyone without management approval. What document-control step was missed?

**Answer:**  
The **Approval** step was missed. The policy should be reviewed and formally approved by the authorized person before official use.
