## Interview Questions

### Q1. Why is the date important in document control?

**Answer:**  
It helps track when a document was created, updated, issued, or approved.

### Q2. What is the difference between Updated Date and Approval Date?

**Answer:**  
**Updated Date** shows when changes were made, while **Approval Date** shows when the updated document was formally approved.

### Q3. Why is the date useful during an audit?

**Answer:**  
It provides evidence of when a document was created, reviewed, updated, or approved.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** An auditor asks when the current version of a security policy was approved. Which document-control information should you check?

**Answer:**  
The **Approval Date**.
