## Interview Questions

### Q1. How should you explain a technical concept in documentation?

**Answer:**  
Explain it clearly using a simple definition, how it works, and a relevant example while maintaining technical accuracy.

### Q2. Why should technical documentation avoid unnecessary jargon?

**Answer:**  
Because the document may be read by both technical and non-technical people, and unnecessary jargon can cause confusion.

### Q3. What should you consider when writing technical documentation?

**Answer:**  
The audience, technical accuracy, clarity, consistency, and purpose of the document.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** You need to explain a firewall to a company's HR team. Should you explain packet filtering, ports, protocols, and stateful inspection in detail immediately?

**Answer:**  
No. First explain the basic purpose of a firewall in simple language and provide a practical example. Add technical details only when they are relevant to the audience.
