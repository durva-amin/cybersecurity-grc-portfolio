## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Security Control?

**Answer:**  
A security control is a measure used to prevent, detect, or reduce security risks.

### Q2. Give examples of network security controls.

**Answer:**  
Firewall, IDS, IPS, VPN, access control, and encryption.

### Q3. What is the purpose of a firewall?

**Answer:**  
A firewall monitors and controls network traffic according to defined security rules.

### Q4. What is the difference between IDS and IPS?

**Answer:**  
**IDS detects and alerts** about suspicious activity, while **IPS can detect and take action to block or prevent it.**

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** A company installs a firewall to block unauthorized network traffic from reaching its internal systems. What is the firewall?

**Answer:**  
A **security control** used to reduce the risk of unauthorized network access.
