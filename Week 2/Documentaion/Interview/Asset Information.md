## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Asset Information?

**Answer:**  
Asset Information is information that identifies and describes an organization's systems, devices, applications, data, and other resources.

### Q2. Why is asset information important in GRC?

**Answer:**  
It helps identify what needs to be protected and supports risk assessments, audits, and security controls.

### Q3. What information can be recorded for a network asset?

**Answer:**  
Asset name, type, IP address, owner, location, purpose, and security status.

### Q4. What is an asset?

**Answer:**  
An asset is anything valuable to an organization that needs to be protected, such as data, systems, devices, applications, or services.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** During a risk assessment, you discover that the company does not know which servers are connected to its network. What documentation is missing?

**Answer:**  
The organization needs proper **asset information/inventory** to identify and track its network assets.
