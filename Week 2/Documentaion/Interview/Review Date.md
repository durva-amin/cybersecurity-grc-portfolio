## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Review Date?

**Answer:**  
A Review Date is the scheduled date when a document should be reviewed to ensure it remains accurate and relevant.

### Q2. Why is a Review Date important in GRC?

**Answer:**  
It helps ensure documentation remains current and supports compliance and audit requirements.

### Q3. Does a Review Date always mean the document must be changed?

**Answer:**  
No. The document is reviewed on that date, but it may remain unchanged if it is still accurate and relevant.

### Q4. Who is responsible for ensuring a document is reviewed?

**Answer:**  
The **Document Owner** is generally responsible for ensuring that the document is reviewed.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** A security policy has reached its Review Date, but there have been no changes in the organization's processes or security requirements. What should happen?

**Answer:**  
The policy should still be reviewed. If it remains accurate and relevant, it can continue to be used, with the review recorded appropriately.
