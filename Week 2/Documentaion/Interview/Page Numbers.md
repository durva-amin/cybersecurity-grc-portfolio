## Interview Questions

### Q1. Why are page numbers used in documentation?

**Answer:**  
Page numbers help readers navigate the document and identify specific pages.

### Q2. Where are page numbers commonly placed?

**Answer:**  
They are commonly placed in the **header or footer**, with the footer being very common.

### Q3. Why are page numbers useful during audits?

**Answer:**  
They allow auditors and employees to easily reference specific pages or sections of a document.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** An auditor asks you to refer to the security control described on page 7 of a 15-page document. What feature helps you locate and reference that page?

**Answer:**  
**Page numbers.**
