## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Document Owner?

**Answer:**  
A Document Owner is the person responsible for maintaining, reviewing, and ensuring the accuracy of a document.

### Q2. Why is a Document Owner important in GRC?

**Answer:**  
It establishes clear accountability for maintaining and reviewing the document.

### Q3. Is the Document Owner always the Author?

**Answer:**  
No. The person who creates a document may be different from the person responsible for owning and maintaining it.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** A security policy has not been reviewed for two years. Who should normally ensure that the policy is reviewed and updated?

**Answer:**  
The **Document Owner** is responsible for ensuring that the document is reviewed and maintained.
