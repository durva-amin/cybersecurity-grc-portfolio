## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a risk?

**Answer:**  
Risk is the possibility that a threat exploits a vulnerability and causes harm to an organization.

### Q2. What is an observation?

**Answer:**  
An observation is a finding or condition identified during a review, assessment, or audit that may require attention.

### Q3. What is the difference between a risk and an observation?

**Answer:**  
An **observation describes what was found**, while a **risk describes the potential negative impact or consequence**.

### Q4. Why are observations important in GRC?

**Answer:**  
They help identify weaknesses or areas requiring attention and can support risk assessment and corrective actions.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** During an audit, you discover that several network devices do not have updated firmware. What is the observation, and what could be the risk?

**Answer:**

**Observation:** Several network devices are running outdated firmware.

**Risk:** Known vulnerabilities in the outdated firmware could be exploited by attackers, potentially compromising the devices or network.
