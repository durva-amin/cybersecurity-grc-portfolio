## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Header?

**Answer:**  
A Header is the area at the top of a document page used to display repeated information.

### Q2. What is a Footer?

**Answer:**  
A Footer is the area at the bottom of a document page used to display repeated information such as page numbers.

### Q3. Why are Headers and Footers useful in GRC documentation?

**Answer:**  
They help identify documents, maintain consistency, and provide useful information such as document title, version, and page numbers.

### Q4. What information can be included in a Footer?

**Answer:**  
Page numbers, document version, classification, or other document-control information.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** An auditor receives individual printed pages of a security policy, but some pages are separated from the document. How can a Header or Footer help?

**Answer:**  
The Header or Footer can contain the document title, version, and page number, making it easier to identify and organize the pages.
