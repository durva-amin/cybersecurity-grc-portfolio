## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Table of Contents?

**Answer:**  
A Table of Contents is a list of the headings and subheadings in a document with their page numbers.

### Q2. How is a Table of Contents created automatically in Word?

**Answer:**  
By applying proper Heading Styles such as Heading 1, Heading 2, and Heading 3 to the document sections.

### Q3. Why is a TOC useful in technical documentation?

**Answer:**  
It helps readers quickly navigate through a long document and find specific sections.

### Q4. What happens if you manually type headings instead of using Heading Styles?

**Answer:**  
Word may not recognize them as headings, so they may not appear correctly in an automatic Table of Contents.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** You have created a 20-page security assessment report. You want Word to automatically generate a list of all major sections and their page numbers. What should you use?

**Answer:**  
A **Table of Contents**, created using proper **Heading Styles**.
