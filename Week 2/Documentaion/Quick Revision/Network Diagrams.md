**Network Diagram = Visual map of a network**

Shows:

- Devices
- Connections
- Network structure
- Security controls

**GRC use → Understand environment + identify controls + assess risks**




### Interview Tip

A network diagram should be **clear and accurate**. Don't add devices or connections that you haven't verified.

### 🔑 Easy Trick to Remember

> **Network Diagram = Who is connected to whom, and how?**
