**Version = Current edition of a document**

- **1.0 → Initial release**
- **1.1 → Minor update**
- **2.0 → Major revision**


### Interview Tip

Don't confuse **Version** with **Version History**.

**Version → Tells you the current version.**

**Version History → Records all previous versions and what changed.**

### 🔑 Easy Trick to Remember

> **Version = Where are we now?**  
> **Version History = How did we get here?**
