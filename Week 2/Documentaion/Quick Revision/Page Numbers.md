**Page Numbers → Show the order of pages.**

Useful for:

- Navigation
- Printed documents
- Audits
- Page references

**Example:** `Page 4 of 10`



## Interview Tip

For a professional security document, avoid decorative page-number designs. Keep them **simple and consistent**.

### 🔑 Easy Trick to Remember

**Page Number = Where am I in the document?** 📄
