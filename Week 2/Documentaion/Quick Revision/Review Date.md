**Review Date = When the document should be checked**

Purpose:

- Check accuracy
- Identify outdated information
- Maintain compliance
- Support audits

**Important:** Review does **not always mean update**.



### Interview Tip

Remember the difference:

**Version → Which version is this?**

**Date → When was it created/updated/approved?**

**Review Date → When should it be checked again?**

### 🔑 Easy Trick to Remember

> **Review Date = "Check again on this date."**
