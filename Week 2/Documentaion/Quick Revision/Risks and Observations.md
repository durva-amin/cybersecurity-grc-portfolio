**Observation → What did we find?**

**Risk → What could go wrong?**

**Impact → What could happen if the risk occurs?**

**Action → What should be done?**




### Interview Tip

In GRC, don't simply say:

> "There is a security problem."

Be specific:

> **Observation → Risk → Impact → Recommended Action**

This makes your finding much more useful for management and audit purposes.

### 🔑 Easy Trick to Remember

**Observation = Fact**

**Risk = Possibility**

**Impact = Consequence**

**Action = Solution**
