**Table = Rows + Columns**

Useful for:

- Network devices
- Controls
- Version history

**Main benefit → Clear + organized + easy comparison**




## Interview Tip

Don't put everything into tables just because they look professional.

Use a table when information has **repeated categories or fields** that need to be compared.

### 🔑 Easy Trick to Remember

**If information can be compared → Think TABLE.** 📊
