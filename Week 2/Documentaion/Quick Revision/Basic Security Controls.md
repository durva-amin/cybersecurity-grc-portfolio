**Security Control = Protection measure**

Examples:

- **Firewall → Controls traffic**
- **IDS → Detects suspicious activity**
- **IPS → Detects and prevents**
- **VPN → Secures network connection**
- **Access Control → Restricts access**
- **Encryption → Protects data**


### Interview Tip

Remember the basic idea:

> **Security controls reduce risk.**

In GRC, don't just identify a control. You should also understand **what risk the control is intended to reduce**.
