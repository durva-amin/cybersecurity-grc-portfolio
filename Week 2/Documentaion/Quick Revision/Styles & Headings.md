**Styles** → Consistent formatting.

**Heading 1** → Main section.

**Heading 2** → Subsection.

**Heading 3** → Sub-subsection.

**Main benefit** → Consistency + structure + automatic Table of Contents.




## ⭐ Interview Tip

Don't confuse **bold text** with a **Heading Style**.

If you simply make text bold and increase its size, Word may treat it as normal text.

If you apply **Heading 1 / Heading 2**, Word understands the document structure.

### 🔑 Easy Trick to Remember

**Heading Styles = Structure**

**Bold/Font Size = Appearance**

For GRC and technical documentation, you want **both**: professional appearance **and** proper document structure.
