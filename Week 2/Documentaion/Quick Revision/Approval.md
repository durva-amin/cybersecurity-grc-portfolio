**Approval = Formal authorization**

It confirms:

- Document was reviewed.
- Authorized person accepted it.
- Document can be officially used.

**Author → Creates**

**Document Owner → Maintains**

**Approver → Authorizes**



## Interview Tip

In GRC, a document being **written** does not automatically make it an **approved document**.

Remember:

> **Created ≠ Approved**
