- **Smishing = SMS Phishing.**
- Uses **text messages (SMS)**.
- Often contains fake links or urgent messages.
- Targets passwords, OTPs, bank details, and personal information.


### Phishing vs Vishing vs Smishing

|Attack|Method|
|---|---|
|**Phishing**|Email, fake websites, messages|
|**Vishing**|Phone calls (Voice)|
|**Smishing**|SMS/Text messages|

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Phishing** → General phishing attack
- **Vishing** → **V = Voice**
- **Smishing** → **S = SMS**
