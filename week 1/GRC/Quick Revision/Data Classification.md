- **Data Classification = Categorizing data by sensitivity.**
- Common levels:
    - **Public** → Anyone can access.
    - **Internal** → Employees only.
    - **Confidential** → Authorized users only.
    - **Restricted** → Highest level of protection.
- Helps improve security and compliance.


### Confidential Data vs Data Classification

- **Confidential Data:** A **type (classification level)** of sensitive data.
- **Data Classification:** The **process** of assigning data to categories such as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Data Classification** → **The process of labeling data.**
- **Confidential** → **One label/category within that process.**
