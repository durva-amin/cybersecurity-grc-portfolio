- **Rootkit = Hides malware.**
- Difficult to detect and remove.
- Can hide files, processes, and other malware.
- May give attackers remote access.


### Rootkit vs Trojan

- **Trojan:** Tricks users into installing malicious software.
- **Rootkit:** Hides malicious software after it is installed.

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Trojan** → **Tricks**
- **Rootkit** → **Hides**
