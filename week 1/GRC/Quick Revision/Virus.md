- **Virus = A type of malware.**
- Attaches to a **file or program**.
- Needs **user action** to spread.
- Can damage files and slow down systems.


### Phishing vs Vishing

|Phishing|Vishing|
|---|---|
|Email, websites, or messages|Phone calls or voice messages|
|Tricks users online|Tricks users over a call|

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Vishing** → **V = Voice**
- **Smishing** (next topic) → **S = SMS**
