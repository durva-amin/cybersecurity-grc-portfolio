# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Rootkit?

**Answer:**  
A Rootkit is a type of malware that hides itself and other malicious programs from users and security software.

---

### Q2. What is the main purpose of a Rootkit?

**Answer:**  
To remain hidden while giving attackers unauthorized access to a system.

---

### Q3. Why is a Rootkit dangerous?

**Answer:**  
Because it is difficult to detect and can hide other malware from security tools.

---

### Q4. How does a Rootkit enter a system?

**Answer:**  
Through infected software, phishing emails, software vulnerabilities, or Trojan infections.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company's antivirus cannot detect malware because it is being hidden by another malicious program. What type of malware is responsible?

**Answer:**  
**Rootkit**, because it hides malware and itself from detection.




### Rootkit vs Trojan

- **Trojan:** Tricks users into installing malicious software.
- **Rootkit:** Hides malicious software after it is installed.

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Trojan** → **Tricks**
- **Rootkit** → **Hides**
