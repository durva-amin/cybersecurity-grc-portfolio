# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Threat?

**Answer:**  
A threat is anything that has the potential to cause harm to a system, network, application, or data.

---

### Q2. Give three examples of threats.

**Answer:**

- Hacker
- Malware
- Flood

---

### Q3. What are the main types of threats?

**Answer:**

- Natural threats
- Human threats
- Environmental threats

---

### Q4. Is every threat harmful?

**Answer:**  
No. A threat is only a **potential danger**. It becomes harmful when it successfully causes damage.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A hacker is trying to break into a company's server but has not succeeded yet. Is this a threat?

**Answer:**  
Yes. The hacker is a **threat** because they have the potential to cause harm, even if the attack has not been successful.
