# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Spyware?

**Answer:**  
Spyware is a type of malware that secretly monitors a user's activities and collects information without their knowledge.

---

### Q2. What is the main purpose of Spyware?

**Answer:**  
To secretly collect sensitive information such as passwords, banking details, and browsing history.

---

### Q3. How does Spyware spread?

**Answer:**  
Through fake software, malicious websites, email attachments, and untrusted downloads.

---

### Q4. What information can Spyware collect?

**Answer:**  
Passwords, usernames, banking information, browsing history, and personal data.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A user's passwords and browsing history are being secretly recorded without their knowledge. What type of malware is this?

**Answer:**  
**Spyware**, because it secretly monitors user activity and steals information.



### Spyware vs Ransomware

- **Spyware:** Secretly **collects information**.
- **Ransomware:** **Encrypts files** and demands money.

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Spyware** → **Spies**
- **Ransomware** → **Ransom** (asks for money)
