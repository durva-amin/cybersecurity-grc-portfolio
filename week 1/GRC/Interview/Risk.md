# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Risk?

**Answer:**  
Risk is the possibility that a threat will exploit a vulnerability and cause harm or loss.

---

### Q2. Give one example of Risk.

**Answer:**  
If a company has weak passwords, there is a risk that an attacker may gain unauthorized access to its systems.

---

### Q3. What can happen if risks are not managed?

**Answer:**  
It can result in data breaches, financial loss, business disruption, and damage to the organization's reputation.

---

### Q4. Can risk exist without a vulnerability?

**Answer:**  
No. A risk exists when there is a vulnerability that a threat can exploit.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company has an unpatched server connected to the internet. What is the risk?

**Answer:**  
The risk is that an attacker may exploit the unpatched server to gain unauthorized access or steal data.
