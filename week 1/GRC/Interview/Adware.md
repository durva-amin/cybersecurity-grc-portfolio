# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Adware?

**Answer:**  
Adware is a type of malware that displays unwanted advertisements on a user's device.

---

### Q2. What is the main purpose of Adware?

**Answer:**  
To display advertisements and often generate revenue for the attacker or software developer.

---

### Q3. How does Adware spread?

**Answer:**  
Through free software, bundled applications, browser extensions, and malicious websites.

---

### Q4. Can Adware collect user information?

**Answer:**  
Yes. Some adware can collect browsing habits and other user information.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. After installing a free application, a user starts seeing many unwanted pop-up advertisements and browser redirects. What type of malware is this?

**Answer:**  
**Adware**, because it displays unwanted advertisements and may change browser settings.
