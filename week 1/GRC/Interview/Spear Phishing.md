# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Spear Phishing?

**Answer:**  
Spear Phishing is a phishing attack that targets a specific person or organization using personalized information.

---

### Q2. How is Spear Phishing different from Phishing?

**Answer:**  
Phishing targets many people with the same message, while Spear Phishing targets a specific person using personalized information.

---

### Q3. Why is Spear Phishing more dangerous?

**Answer:**  
Because it is personalized, making it more convincing and increasing the chances that the victim will trust it.

---

### Q4. How can you protect yourself from Spear Phishing?

**Answer:**

- Verify the sender.
- Be cautious with unexpected emails.
- Do not open unknown attachments.
- Confirm requests through another communication method.
- Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. An attacker sends a personalized email to the Finance Manager using their name and job title, asking them to transfer money. What type of attack is this?

**Answer:**  
**Spear Phishing**, because the attack is targeted at a specific individual using personalized information.
