### Q1. What is the CIA Triad?

**Answer:**  
The CIA Triad is a security model consisting of **Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability**, which are the three core principles of Information Security.

---

### Q2. What is Confidentiality?

**Answer:**  
Confidentiality ensures that only authorized users can access information.

---

### Q3. What is Integrity?

**Answer:**  
Integrity ensures that information remains accurate and is not modified without authorization.

---

### Q4. What is Availability?

**Answer:**  
Availability ensures that systems and information are accessible whenever authorized users need them.

---

### Q5. Give one example of each principle.

**Answer:**

- **Confidentiality:** Employee salary records accessible only to HR.
- **Integrity:** Exam marks cannot be altered by unauthorized users.
- **Availability:** ATM services are available 24×7.


# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A hacker changes a company's customer database without permission. Which part of the CIA Triad is affected?

**Answer:**  
**Integrity**, because the data was modified without authorization.
