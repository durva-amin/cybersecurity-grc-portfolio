# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is an Attack Surface?

**Answer:**  
An attack surface is the total number of possible entry points that an attacker can use to attack a system.

---

### Q2. What are the three types of Attack Surface?

**Answer:**

- Digital
- Physical
- Human

---

### Q3. Why should an organization reduce its Attack Surface?

**Answer:**  
Because fewer entry points mean fewer opportunities for attackers to compromise the system.

---

### Q4. Give two examples of a Digital Attack Surface.

**Answer:**

- Website
- Mobile application

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company removes unused user accounts and closes unused network ports. What security goal are they achieving?

**Answer:**  
They are **reducing the attack surface**, making it harder for attackers to find entry points.


**"What is the difference between an Attack Surface and a Vulnerability?"**

**Answer:**

- **Attack Surface:** All possible places an attacker can try to enter.
- **Vulnerability:** A specific weakness in one of those places.

**Easy Example:**

- Company website = **Attack Surface**
- Outdated website software = **Vulnerability**
