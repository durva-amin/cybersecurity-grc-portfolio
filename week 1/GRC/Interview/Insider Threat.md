# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is an Insider Threat?

**Answer:**  
An Insider Threat is a security risk caused by a person inside an organization who has authorized access to systems or data.

---

### Q2. What are the three types of Insider Threats?

**Answer:**

- Malicious Insider
- Negligent Insider
- Compromised Insider

---

### Q3. Give one example of a Negligent Insider.

**Answer:**  
An employee accidentally clicks a phishing link or shares their password.

---

### Q4. Why are Insider Threats dangerous?

**Answer:**  
Because insiders already have authorized access, making it easier for them or someone using their account to access sensitive systems and data.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. An employee accidentally clicks a phishing email, allowing attackers to access the company's network. What type of Insider Threat is this?

**Answer:**  
**Negligent Insider Threat**, because the employee unintentionally caused the security incident.
