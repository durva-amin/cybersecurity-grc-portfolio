# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Confidential Data?

**Answer:**  
Confidential Data is sensitive information that should only be accessed by authorized people because unauthorized disclosure can cause harm.

---

### Q2. Give three examples of Confidential Data.

**Answer:**

- Employee salary records
- Customer database
- Business contracts

---

### Q3. Why should Confidential Data be protected?

**Answer:**  
To prevent unauthorized access, financial loss, legal issues, and damage to the organization's reputation.

---

### Q4. How can organizations protect Confidential Data?

**Answer:**  
By using encryption, access control, strong passwords, MFA, and regular backups.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. An employee shares the company's confidential financial report with an unauthorized person. What type of data has been exposed?

**Answer:**  
**Confidential Data**, because sensitive company information was disclosed to someone who was not authorized to access it.
