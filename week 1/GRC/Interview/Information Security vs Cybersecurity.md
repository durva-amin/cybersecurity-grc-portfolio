

### Q1. What is Information Security?

**Answer:**  
Information Security is the practice of protecting information in any form (digital, paper, or verbal) from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

---

### Q2. What is Cybersecurity?

**Answer:**  
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting digital systems, networks, devices, and data from cyber attacks and unauthorized access.

---

### Q3. What is the difference between Information Security and Cybersecurity?

**Answer:**  
Information Security protects information in all forms, while Cybersecurity focuses only on protecting digital systems and digital data. Cybersecurity is a subset of Information Security.

---

### Q4. Which is broader: Information Security or Cybersecurity?

**Answer:**  
Information Security is broader because it covers all forms of information, whereas Cybersecurity only focuses on digital assets.

---

## Interview Scenario

**Q. A company's confidential contract is stolen from a locked filing cabinet. Is this a Cybersecurity issue?**

**Answer:**  
No. This is an **Information Security** issue because the information was stored in a physical (paper) form, not in a digital system.
