# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Data Classification?

**Answer:**  
Data Classification is the process of categorizing data based on its sensitivity and the level of protection it requires.

---

### Q2. Why is Data Classification important?

**Answer:**  
It helps protect sensitive information, control access, and meet compliance requirements.

---

### Q3. Name the common data classification levels.

**Answer:**

- Public
- Internal
- Confidential
- Restricted

---

### Q4. Give one example of Confidential data.

**Answer:**  
Employee salary records or a customer database.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company marks its encryption keys as "Restricted" and allows only a few administrators to access them. What security practice is being followed?

**Answer:**  
**Data Classification**, because the data has been categorized based on its sensitivity and access requirements.
