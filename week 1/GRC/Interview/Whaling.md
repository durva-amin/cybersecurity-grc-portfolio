# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Whaling?

**Answer:**  
Whaling is a type of Spear Phishing that targets high-level executives or senior officials in an organization.

---

### Q2. Who are the common targets of Whaling?

**Answer:**

- CEO
- CFO
- Directors
- Senior Managers

---

### Q3. Why are executives targeted in Whaling attacks?

**Answer:**  
Because they have access to sensitive information and the authority to approve financial transactions.

---

### Q4. How can organizations prevent Whaling attacks?

**Answer:**

- Verify financial requests.
- Train employees and executives.
- Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Confirm requests through another communication method.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A fake email pretending to be from the Board of Directors asks the CEO to urgently transfer funds. What type of attack is this?

**Answer:**  
**Whaling**, because it targets a senior executive.


### Phishing vs Spear Phishing vs Whaling

|Phishing|Spear Phishing|Whaling|
|---|---|---|
|Targets many people|Targets a specific person|Targets top executives|
|Generic message|Personalized message|Personalized message for senior officials|

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Phishing** → Everyone
- **Spear Phishing** → One specific person
- **Whaling** → The "big fish" (CEO, CFO, Directors)
