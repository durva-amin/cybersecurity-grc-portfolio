# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Accounting in AAA?

**Answer:**  
Accounting is the process of recording and monitoring a user's activities after they have accessed a system.

---

### Q2. Why is Accounting important?

**Answer:**  
It helps in auditing, monitoring user activities, detecting misuse, and investigating security incidents.

---

### Q3. What information does Accounting record?

**Answer:**

- Login/logout time
- User ID
- Files accessed
- Actions performed
- Date and time of activities

---

### Q4. Give one example of Accounting.

**Answer:**  
A system records that a user logged in at 9:00 AM and downloaded a confidential document at 9:15 AM.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company wants to know who deleted an important file and at what time. Which part of AAA helps?

**Answer:**  
**Accounting**, because it records user activities and maintains logs.
