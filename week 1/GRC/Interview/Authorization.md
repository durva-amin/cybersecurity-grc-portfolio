### Q1. What is Authorization?

**Answer:**  
Authorization is the process of determining what an authenticated user is allowed to access or perform.

---

### Q2. When does Authorization occur?

**Answer:**  
Authorization occurs **after Authentication**.

---

### Q3. What is the purpose of Authorization?

**Answer:**  
To ensure users can only access the data and resources they are permitted to use.

---

### Q4. Give an example of Authorization.

**Answer:**  
After logging into a company's system, an HR employee can access salary records, while a Sales employee cannot.

---

### Q5. Can a user be authenticated but not authorized?

**Answer:**  
Yes. A user may successfully log in (authenticated) but still not have permission to access certain files or features.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. An employee successfully logs into the company portal but receives an "Access Denied" message when opening the Finance folder. Which security concept is involved?

**Answer:**  
**Authorization**, because the user is authenticated but does not have permission to access the Finance folder.
