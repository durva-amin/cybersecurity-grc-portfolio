# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Vishing?

**Answer:**  
Vishing is a phishing attack where attackers use phone calls or voice messages to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

---

### Q2. What does the "V" in Vishing stand for?

**Answer:**  
**Voice**.

---

### Q3. What information do attackers try to steal through Vishing?

**Answer:**  
Bank details, passwords, OTPs, card details, and personal information.

---

### Q4. How can you protect yourself from Vishing?

**Answer:**

- Never share OTPs or passwords over the phone.
- Verify the caller's identity.
- Disconnect suspicious calls.
- Contact the organization using its official phone number.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. You receive a phone call from someone claiming to be from your bank asking for your OTP to verify your account. What type of attack is this?

**Answer:**  
**Vishing**, because the attacker is using a phone call to steal sensitive information.


### Phishing vs Vishing

|Phishing|Vishing|
|---|---|
|Email, websites, or messages|Phone calls or voice messages|
|Tricks users online|Tricks users over a call|

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Vishing** → **V = Voice**
- **Smishing** (next topic) → **S = SMS**
