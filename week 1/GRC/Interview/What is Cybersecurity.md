
**Q1. What is Cybersecurity?**

**Answer:** Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting digital systems, networks, and data from cyber attacks and unauthorized access.

**Q2. Why is Cybersecurity important?**

**Answer:** It protects sensitive data, prevents attacks, reduces financial loss, and ensures business continuity.

**Q3. Give one real-life example of Cybersecurity.**

**Answer:** Online banking uses cybersecurity to protect customer accounts and financial transactions.
