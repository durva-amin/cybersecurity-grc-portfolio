# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Personal Data?

**Answer:**  
Personal Data is any information that can directly or indirectly identify an individual.

---

### Q2. Give five examples of Personal Data.

**Answer:**

- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Aadhaar Number
- Date of Birth

---

### Q3. Why should Personal Data be protected?

**Answer:**  
To protect people's privacy, prevent identity theft, financial fraud, and unauthorized access.

---

### Q4. Is an email address considered Personal Data?

**Answer:**  
Yes. An email address can identify a person, so it is Personal Data.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company accidentally exposes customers' names, phone numbers, and email addresses online. What type of data has been leaked?

**Answer:**  
**Personal Data**, because the leaked information can identify individuals.
