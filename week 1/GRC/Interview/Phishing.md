# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Phishing?

**Answer:**  
Phishing is a cyber attack in which attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information by pretending to be a trusted source.

---

### Q2. What information do attackers try to steal through Phishing?

**Answer:**  
Passwords, usernames, bank details, credit card information, OTPs, and personal information.

---

### Q3. How is Phishing commonly carried out?

**Answer:**  
Through fake emails, fake websites, SMS messages, and social media messages.

---

### Q4. How can you protect yourself from Phishing?

**Answer:**

- Verify the sender.
- Do not click suspicious links.
- Check the website URL.
- Never share passwords or OTPs.
- Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. You receive an email saying your bank account will be blocked unless you click a link and log in immediately. What type of attack is this?

**Answer:**  
**Phishing**, because the attacker is pretending to be a trusted organization to steal your information.



**Phishing vs Malware**

- **Phishing:** Tricks the user into giving information.
- **Malware:** Harmful software that infects a device.

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Phishing** → **Tricks people**
- **Malware** → **Infects systems**
