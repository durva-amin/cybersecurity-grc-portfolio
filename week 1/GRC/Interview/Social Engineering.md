# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Social Engineering?

**Answer:**  
Social Engineering is a technique where attackers manipulate people into revealing sensitive information or performing actions that compromise security.

---

### Q2. What is the main goal of Social Engineering?

**Answer:**  
To trick people into sharing confidential information or giving unauthorized access.

---

### Q3. Name four examples of Social Engineering attacks.

**Answer:**

- Phishing
- Spear Phishing
- Vishing
- Smishing

---

### Q4. How can you protect yourself from Social Engineering?

**Answer:**

- Verify the identity of the person.
- Never share passwords or OTPs.
- Be cautious of urgent requests.
- Report suspicious emails, calls, or messages.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. Someone pretending to be from the IT team asks an employee for their password over the phone. What type of attack is this?

**Answer:**  
**Social Engineering**, because the attacker is manipulating the employee into revealing confidential information.



### Social Engineering vs Phishing

- **Social Engineering:** The **overall technique** of manipulating people.
- **Phishing:** **One type** of social engineering attack, usually using emails or fake websites.

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Social Engineering = Category (tricking people)**
- **Phishing = One method of social engineering**
