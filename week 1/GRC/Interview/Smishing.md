# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Smishing?

**Answer:**  
Smishing is a phishing attack where attackers use SMS or text messages to trick users into revealing sensitive information or clicking malicious links.

---

### Q2. What does the "S" in Smishing stand for?

**Answer:**  
**SMS**.

---

### Q3. What information do attackers try to steal through Smishing?

**Answer:**  
Passwords, OTPs, bank details, card details, and personal information.

---

### Q4. How can you protect yourself from Smishing?

**Answer:**

- Do not click suspicious links in SMS.
- Verify the sender.
- Never share OTPs or passwords.
- Contact the organization through official channels.

---

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. You receive an SMS claiming you've won a prize and asking you to click a link and enter your bank details. What type of attack is this?

**Answer:**  
**Smishing**, because the attacker is using an SMS to trick the victim.




### Phishing vs Vishing vs Smishing

|Attack|Method|
|---|---|
|**Phishing**|Email, fake websites, messages|
|**Vishing**|Phone calls (Voice)|
|**Smishing**|SMS/Text messages|

**Easy Trick to Remember:**

- **Phishing** → General phishing attack
- **Vishing** → **V = Voice**
- **Smishing** → **S = SMS**
