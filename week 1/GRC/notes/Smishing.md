## Definition

**Smishing (SMS Phishing)** is a type of **phishing attack where attackers use SMS (text messages) to trick people into revealing sensitive information or clicking malicious links.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Smishing is **phishing through SMS or text messages.**

---

## Purpose of Smishing

Attackers try to steal:

- Passwords
- OTPs
- Bank account details
- Credit/Debit card details
- Personal information

---

## How Does Smishing Work?

1. The attacker sends a fake SMS.
2. The SMS contains a malicious link or asks for personal information.
3. The victim clicks the link or shares the information.
4. The attacker steals the information.
