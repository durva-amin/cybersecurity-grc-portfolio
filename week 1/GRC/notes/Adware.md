## Definition

**Adware** is a type of **malware** that **automatically displays unwanted advertisements on a user's device.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Adware is software that **shows unwanted ads** on your computer or mobile device.

---

## Characteristics of Adware

- Displays unwanted advertisements.
- Slows down the system.
- May redirect users to unwanted websites.
- Sometimes collects browsing information.

---

## What Can Adware Do?

- Show pop-up ads.
- Change the browser homepage.
- Redirect search results.
- Track browsing habits.
- Reduce system performance.

---

## How Does Adware Enter a System?

- Free software downloads
- Bundled software
- Malicious websites
- Browser extensions
