## Definition

A **Threat** is **anything that has the potential to cause harm to a system, network, application, or data**.

**Simple Definition:**

> A threat is a **possible danger** that can exploit a weakness and cause damage.

---

## Why are Threats Dangerous?

Threats can:

- Steal data
- Damage systems
- Disrupt business operations
- Cause financial loss
- Harm an organization's reputation

---

## Types of Threats

### 1. Natural Threats

Caused by nature.

**Examples:**

- Flood
- Earthquake
- Fire
- Lightning

---

### 2. Human Threats

Caused by people.

**Examples:**

- Hacker
- Insider
- Cybercriminal
- Employee mistake

---

### 3. Environmental Threats

Caused by the surrounding environment.

**Examples:**

- Power failure
- Hardware failure
- Air conditioning failure (server room overheating)
