## Definition

**Risk** is the **possibility that a threat will exploit a vulnerability and cause harm or loss**.

**Simple Definition:**

> Risk is the **chance of damage or loss** when a threat exploits a vulnerability.

---

## Why is Risk Important?

Risk can lead to:

- Financial loss
- Data loss
- Business disruption
- Reputation damage
- Legal or compliance issues

---

## Examples of Risk

- Customer data being stolen
- Website becoming unavailable
- Financial fraud
- Loss of confidential information
