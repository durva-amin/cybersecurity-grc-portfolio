## Definition

**Data Classification** is the process of **organizing data into categories based on its sensitivity and the level of protection it requires.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Data Classification means **grouping data according to how sensitive it is.**

---

## Why is Data Classification Important?

- Protects sensitive information.
- Controls who can access data.
- Helps organizations meet legal and compliance requirements.
- Reduces the risk of data leaks.

---

## Common Data Classification Levels

### 1. Public

Information that anyone can access.

**Examples:**

- Company website
- Public advertisements
- Press releases

---

### 2. Internal

Information meant only for employees.

**Examples:**

- Internal policies
- Employee directory
- Meeting schedules

---

### 3. Confidential

Sensitive information that should only be accessed by authorized people.

**Examples:**

- Employee salary records
- Customer database
- Business contracts

---

### 4. Restricted (Highly Confidential)

The most sensitive information that requires the highest level of protection.

**Examples:**

- Encryption keys
- Military information
- Product source code
- Merger and acquisition documents
