## Definition

**Vishing (Voice Phishing)** is a type of **phishing attack where attackers use phone calls or voice messages to trick people into revealing sensitive information.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Vishing is **phishing through a phone call.**

---

## Purpose of Vishing

Attackers try to steal:

- Bank account details
- ATM/Debit card details
- Credit card details
- Passwords
- OTPs
- Personal information

---

## How Does Vishing Work?

1. The attacker calls the victim pretending to be from a bank, police, or a trusted company.
2. The attacker creates a sense of urgency or fear.
3. The victim shares confidential information.
4. The attacker misuses the information.
