## Definition

**Authentication** is the process of **verifying the identity of a user, device, or system** before allowing access.

**Simple Definition:**

> Authentication answers the question: **"Who are you?"**

---

## Why is Authentication Important?

- Prevents unauthorized access.
- Protects sensitive information.
- Ensures only verified users can log in.

---

## Types (Factors) of Authentication

### 1. Something You Know

Information only the user knows.

**Examples:**

- Password
- PIN
- Security Question

---

### 2. Something You Have

An item the user possesses.

**Examples:**

- OTP on mobile
- Smart Card
- Security Token
- Authenticator App

---

### 3. Something You Are

A unique biological characteristic.

**Examples:**

- Fingerprint
- Face Recognition
- Iris Scan
- Voice Recognition

---

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

Using **two or more authentication factors** to verify a user's identity.

**Example:**

- Password + OTP
- Fingerprint + PIN
