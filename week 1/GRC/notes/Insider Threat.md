## Definition

An **Insider Threat** is a **security risk caused by a person inside an organization who has authorized access to its systems, data, or resources.**

**Simple Definition:**

> An Insider Threat is **a trusted person inside the organization who can cause harm.**

---

## Types of Insider Threats

### 1. Malicious Insider

A person who **intentionally** causes harm.

**Examples:**

- Stealing company data
- Selling confidential information
- Deleting important files

---

### 2. Negligent Insider

A person who causes harm **by mistake or carelessness**.

**Examples:**

- Clicking a phishing email
- Sharing a password
- Losing a company laptop

---

### 3. Compromised Insider

A person's account is **taken over by an attacker**.

**Example:**

- An attacker steals an employee's login credentials and uses the account to access company data.

---

## Who Can Be an Insider?

- Employees
- Managers
- Contractors
- Vendors
- Interns
