## Definition

**Spear Phishing** is a type of **phishing attack that targets a specific person or organization using personalized information.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Spear Phishing is **a targeted phishing attack aimed at a specific person.**

---

## Characteristics of Spear Phishing

- Targets a specific individual or organization.
- Uses personal information to appear genuine.
- More convincing than regular phishing.
- Aims to steal sensitive information or install malware.

---

## How Does Spear Phishing Work?

1. The attacker gathers information about the victim.
2. Sends a personalized email or message.
3. The victim trusts the message.
4. The victim clicks a link, opens an attachment, or shares sensitive information.
