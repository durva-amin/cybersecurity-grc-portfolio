## Definition

The **CIA Triad** is the foundation of Information Security. It consists of **three security principles** used to protect information and systems.

- **C – Confidentiality**
- **I – Integrity**
- **A – Availability**

---

## 1. Confidentiality

**Meaning:**  
Information should be accessed **only by authorized people**.

**Example:**  
Only the HR department can view employee salary details.

**How it is achieved:**

- Passwords
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Encryption
- Access Control

---

## 2. Integrity

**Meaning:**  
Information should remain **accurate, complete, and unchanged** unless modified by an authorized person.

**Example:**  
A student's exam marks should not be changed by another student.

**How it is achieved:**

- Hashing
- Digital Signatures
- Version Control
- Access Control

---

## 3. Availability

**Meaning:**  
Information and systems should be **available whenever authorized users need them**.

**Example:**  
Online banking should be available 24×7.

**How it is achieved:**

- Regular Backups
- Disaster Recovery Plan
- Redundant Servers
- UPS/Power Backup
