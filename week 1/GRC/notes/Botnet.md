## Definition

A **Botnet** is a **network of infected computers or devices (called bots or zombies) that are controlled remotely by an attacker without the owners' knowledge.**

**Simple Definition:**

> A Botnet is a **group of infected devices controlled by a hacker.**

---

## Characteristics of a Botnet

- Consists of many infected devices.
- Controlled remotely by an attacker.
- Device owners are usually unaware.
- Used to perform large-scale cyber attacks.

---

## What Can a Botnet Do?

- Launch DDoS attacks
- Send spam emails
- Spread malware
- Steal data
- Mine cryptocurrency

---

## How Does a Device Become Part of a Botnet?

- Malware infection
- Phishing emails
- Malicious downloads
- Software vulnerabilities
