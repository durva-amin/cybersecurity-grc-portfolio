## Definition

**Personal Data** is **any information that can identify a person, either directly or indirectly.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Personal Data is **information that identifies an individual.**

---

## Examples of Personal Data

- Name
- Address
- Phone number
- Email address
- Date of Birth
- Aadhaar Number
- PAN Number
- Passport Number
- Driving License Number
- Photograph

---

## Sensitive Personal Data (Examples)

Some personal data is more sensitive and requires stronger protection.

Examples:

- Biometric data (Fingerprint, Face Scan, Iris Scan)
- Financial information
- Medical records
- Passwords

---

## Why is Personal Data Important?

If personal data is leaked, it can lead to:

- Identity theft
- Financial fraud
- Privacy violations
- Reputation damage

---

## How to Protect Personal Data?

- Use strong passwords
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Encrypt sensitive data
- Share information only when necessary
- Restrict access to authorized users
