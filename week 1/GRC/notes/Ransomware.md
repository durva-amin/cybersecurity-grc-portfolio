## Definition

**Ransomware** is a type of **malware** that **encrypts a victim's files or locks their system and demands money (ransom) to restore access.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Ransomware locks or encrypts your files and asks for money to unlock them.

---

## Characteristics of Ransomware

- Encrypts files or locks the system.
- Demands a ransom payment.
- May threaten to delete or leak data.
- Can affect individuals and organizations.

---

## How Does Ransomware Spread?

- Phishing emails
- Malicious attachments
- Fake software downloads
- Infected websites
- Unpatched software vulnerabilities
