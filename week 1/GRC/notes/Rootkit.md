## Definition

**Rootkit** is a type of **malware** that **hides itself and other malicious programs from users and security software.**

**Simple Definition:**

> A Rootkit is malware that **hides itself to avoid detection.**

---

## Characteristics of a Rootkit

- Hides its presence.
- Gives attackers unauthorized access.
- Can hide other malware.
- Difficult to detect and remove.

---

## What Can a Rootkit Do?

- Hide malicious files and processes.
- Disable security software.
- Give attackers remote access.
- Steal sensitive information.

---

## How Does a Rootkit Enter a System?

- Infected software
- Phishing emails
- Software vulnerabilities
- Trojan infections
