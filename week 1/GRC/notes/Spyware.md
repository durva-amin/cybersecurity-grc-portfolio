## Definition

**Spyware** is a type of **malware** that **secretly monitors a user's activities and collects information without their knowledge or permission.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Spyware secretly **spies on users and steals their information.**

---

## Characteristics of Spyware

- Runs secretly in the background.
- Monitors user activity.
- Collects personal or sensitive information.
- Sends the collected data to an attacker.

---

## What Can Spyware Steal?

- Usernames
- Passwords
- Banking details
- Browsing history
- Personal information

---

## How Does Spyware Enter a System?

- Fake software downloads
- Malicious websites
- Email attachments
- Free software from untrusted sources
