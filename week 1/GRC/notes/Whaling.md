## Definition

**Whaling** is a type of **Spear Phishing** that **targets high-level executives or senior officials** in an organization.

**Simple Definition:**

> Whaling is **a phishing attack that targets CEOs, Directors, or other top executives.**

---

## Who Are the Targets?

- CEO
- CFO
- Managing Director
- Company Owner
- Senior Managers

---

## Purpose of Whaling

Attackers try to:

- Steal confidential information
- Obtain financial data
- Trick executives into transferring money
- Steal login credentials

---

## How Does Whaling Work?

1. The attacker researches the executive.
2. Sends a personalized email pretending to be a trusted person.
3. The executive believes the email is genuine.
4. The executive shares information or transfers money.
