## Definition

**Accounting** (also called **Accounting/Auditing** in AAA) is the process of **recording and tracking what a user does after logging into a system**.

**Simple Definition:**

> Accounting answers the question: **"What did the user do?"**

---

## Purpose of Accounting

- Keeps a record of user activities.
- Helps in auditing and investigations.
- Detects misuse or suspicious activities.
- Maintains accountability.

---

## What Does Accounting Record?

It records details such as:

- User ID
- Login and logout time
- Files accessed
- Commands executed
- Changes made
- Date and time of activities
