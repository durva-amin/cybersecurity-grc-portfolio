## Definition

**Confidential Data** is **sensitive information that is accessible only to authorized people because unauthorized disclosure could cause harm to an individual or organization.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Confidential Data is **information that must be kept secret.**

---

## Why is Confidential Data Important?

If it is leaked, it can cause:

- Financial loss
- Reputation damage
- Legal or compliance issues
- Loss of customer trust

---

## Examples of Confidential Data

- Employee salary details
- Customer database
- Business contracts
- Trade secrets
- Company financial reports
- Source code
- Business strategies

---

## How to Protect Confidential Data?

- Strong passwords
- Encryption
- Access control (only authorized users)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Regular backups
