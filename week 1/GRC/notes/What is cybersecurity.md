### Definition

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems, and digital data from cyber attacks, unauthorized access, theft, or damage.

### Why is Cybersecurity Important?

- Protects sensitive information
- Prevents cyber attacks
- Reduces financial loss
- Maintains customer trust
- Ensures business continuity

### Where is Cybersecurity Used?

- Banks
- Hospitals
- Government
- IT Companies
- E-commerce
- Schools & Universities

### What Does Cybersecurity Protect?

- Computers
- Networks
- Servers
- Applications
- Digital Data
- Cloud Systems
