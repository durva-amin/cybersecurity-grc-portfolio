## Definition

**Social Engineering** is a technique in which **attackers manipulate or trick people into revealing sensitive information or performing actions that compromise security.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Social Engineering is **tricking people instead of hacking computers.**

---

## Purpose of Social Engineering

Attackers use social engineering to:

- Steal passwords
- Obtain OTPs
- Access confidential information
- Install malware
- Gain unauthorized access

---

## Common Social Engineering Attacks

- Phishing
- Spear Phishing
- Whaling
- Vishing
- Smishing

---

## How Does Social Engineering Work?

1. The attacker gains the victim's trust.
2. The attacker creates urgency, fear, or curiosity.
3. The victim shares sensitive information or performs an action.
4. The attacker misuses the information.
