## Definition

**Phishing** is a **cyber attack in which an attacker tricks people into revealing sensitive information by pretending to be a trusted person or organization.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Phishing is **a fake message or email sent to steal your personal information.**

---

## Purpose of Phishing

Attackers use phishing to steal:

- Usernames
- Passwords
- Bank details
- Credit card information
- OTPs
- Personal information

---

## How Does Phishing Work?

1. The attacker sends a fake email, message, or website.
2. The victim believes it is genuine.
3. The victim enters personal information or clicks a malicious link.
4. The attacker steals the information.

---

## Common Phishing Methods

- Fake emails
- Fake websites
- SMS messages
- Social media messages
