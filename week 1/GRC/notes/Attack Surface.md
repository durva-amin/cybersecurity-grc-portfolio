## Definition

An **Attack Surface** is the **total number of points (entry points) where an attacker can try to enter or attack a system.**

**Simple Definition:**

> Attack Surface is **all the possible ways an attacker can attack a system.**

---

## Why is Attack Surface Important?

- A **larger attack surface = Higher security risk.**
- A **smaller attack surface = Better security.**

---

## Types of Attack Surface

### 1. Digital Attack Surface

Entry points in digital systems.

**Examples:**

- Websites
- Applications
- Open ports
- Email accounts
- Cloud services
- APIs

---

### 2. Physical Attack Surface

Physical assets that attackers can access.

**Examples:**

- Laptops
- Servers
- USB drives
- Office computers

---

### 3. Human Attack Surface

People who can be targeted by attackers.

**Examples:**

- Employees
- Customers
- Vendors
- Contractors
