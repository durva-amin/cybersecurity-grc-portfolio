## Definition

### Information Security (InfoSec)

Information Security is the practice of protecting **information in any form** (digital, paper, or verbal) from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

### Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting **digital systems, networks, devices, and data** from cyber attacks and unauthorized access.

---

## Key Difference

|Information Security|Cybersecurity|
|---|---|
|Protects all types of information|Protects digital systems and digital data|
|Covers digital, paper, and verbal information|Covers only digital information|
|Broader concept|Part of Information Security|

**Easy to Remember:**

> **Information Security is the umbrella, and Cybersecurity is one part of it.**
