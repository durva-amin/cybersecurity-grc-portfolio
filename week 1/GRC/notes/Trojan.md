## Definition

A **Trojan** is a type of **malware** that **disguises itself as legitimate or useful software to trick users into installing it.**

**Simple Definition:**

> A Trojan is **fake software that looks genuine but contains malicious code.**

---

## Characteristics of a Trojan

- Looks like a trusted program.
- Tricks users into installing it.
- Does **not** spread by itself.
- Can give attackers unauthorized access.

---

## What Can a Trojan Do?

- Steal data
- Install other malware
- Spy on users
- Give hackers remote access
- Delete or modify files

---

## How Does a Trojan Enter a System?

- Fake software downloads
- Cracked or pirated software
- Email attachments
- Malicious websites
