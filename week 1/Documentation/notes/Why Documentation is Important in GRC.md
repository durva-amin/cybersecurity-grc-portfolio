## Definition

Documentation is the foundation of **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)**. It records policies, processes, risks, controls, and compliance activities.

### Simple Definition

Documentation helps organizations **manage security, reduce risks, and prove compliance**.

---

## Why is Documentation Important in GRC?

- Supports **Governance** by defining security rules and responsibilities.
- Helps identify, assess, and manage **Risks**.
- Provides evidence for **Compliance** with laws, regulations, and standards.
- Helps during internal and external audits.
- Ensures consistency across the organization.
- Tracks changes and improvements over time.

---

## How Documentation Supports GRC

### Governance (G)

- Defines security policies and responsibilities.
- Ensures employees follow organizational rules.

### Risk Management (R)

- Records identified risks.
- Documents risk assessments and treatment plans.

### Compliance (C)

- Demonstrates compliance with laws, regulations, and standards.
- Provides evidence during audits.
