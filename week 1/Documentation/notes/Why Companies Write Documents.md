## Definition

Companies write documents to record rules, processes, responsibilities, and guidelines so everyone works consistently and securely.

### Simple Definition

Companies write documents to ensure everyone follows the same instructions and security practices.

---

## Why Do Companies Write Documents?

- Ensures consistency across the organization.
- Defines roles and responsibilities.
- Helps employees understand what to do.
- Supports audits and compliance.
- Reduces security risks and human errors.
- Preserves knowledge even if employees leave.
- Meets legal and regulatory requirements.

---

## Common Documents Companies Write

- Policies
- Procedures
- Standards
- Guidelines
- Risk Assessment Reports
- Audit Reports
- Incident Response Plans
- Business Continuity Plans
