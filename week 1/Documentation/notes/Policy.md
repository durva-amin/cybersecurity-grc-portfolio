## Definition

A **Policy** is a high-level document that defines an organization's rules, objectives, and expectations regarding a specific area.

### Simple Definition

A **Policy** tells **what must be done**, not **how to do it**.

---

## Why is a Policy Important?

- Sets organizational rules.
- Defines security expectations.
- Ensures consistency.
- Supports compliance.
- Provides direction for employees and management.

---

## Characteristics of a Policy

- High-level document.
- Approved by senior management.
- Mandatory to follow.
- Does not contain detailed steps.
- Applies across the organization.

---

## Examples of Policies

- Information Security Policy
- Password Policy
- Access Control Policy
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Data Classification Policy
- Remote Work Policy
