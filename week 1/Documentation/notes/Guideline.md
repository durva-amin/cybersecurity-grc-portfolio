## Definition

A **Guideline** is a document that provides **recommended best practices** to help employees perform tasks effectively. Unlike policies, procedures, and standards, guidelines are generally **not mandatory** unless the organization specifically requires them.

### Simple Definition

A **Guideline** gives **recommended ways** to do something.

---

## Why is a Guideline Important?

- Recommends best practices.
- Helps employees make good decisions.
- Improves efficiency and security.
- Provides flexibility.
- Supports policies, procedures, and standards.

---

## Characteristics of a Guideline

- Recommended, not usually mandatory.
- Flexible.
- Based on best practices.
- Helps employees make informed decisions.
- Can vary depending on the situation.

---

## Examples of Guidelines

- Password Creation Guideline
- Email Security Guideline
- Remote Work Guideline
- Clean Desk Guideline
- Secure Browsing Guideline
- Social Media Usage Guideline
