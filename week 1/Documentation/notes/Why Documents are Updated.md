## Definition

Documents are updated to ensure they remain **accurate, relevant, and compliant** with current business, security, and legal requirements.

### Simple Definition

Documents are updated because **rules, risks, and requirements change over time.**

---

## Why are Documents Updated?

- Business processes change.
- New security threats emerge.
- Laws and regulations change.
- Audit findings require improvements.
- New technologies are introduced.
- Errors or outdated information need correction.
- Organizational policies are revised.

---

## Common Reasons for Updating Documents

- New company policy
- New cybersecurity threat
- Software or system upgrade
- Compliance or legal changes
- Audit recommendations
- Process improvements
