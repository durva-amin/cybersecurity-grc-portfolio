## Definition

A **Professional Document** is a well-structured document that presents information in a clear, organized, and standardized format.

### Simple Definition

A professional document is a document with a **fixed structure** that makes it easy to read, understand, and manage.

---

## Basic Structure of a Professional Document

### 1. Title

The name of the document.

**Example:**  
Information Security Terms Glossary

---

### 2. Purpose

Explains **why** the document was created.

**Example:**  
To provide basic cybersecurity terms for learning and awareness.

---

### 3. Scope

Defines **who or what** the document applies to.

**Example:**  
This document applies to students learning cybersecurity fundamentals.

---

### 4. Version

Shows the current version of the document.

**Example:**  
Version 1.0

---

### 5. Author

The person who created or updated the document.

**Example:**  
Durva Amin

---

### 6. Content

The main information contained in the document.

**Example:**  
Cybersecurity terms and their definitions.

---

### 7. Review Date

The date when the document should be reviewed or updated.

**Example:**  
04 August 2027

---

## Why is a Professional Document Structure Important?

- Makes documents easy to read.
- Ensures consistency across all documents.
- Helps during audits.
- Makes updates easier.
- Clearly identifies document ownership and version.
