## Definition

A **Standard** is a document that defines **specific mandatory requirements** that must be followed to ensure consistency and security.

### Simple Definition

A **Standard** specifies **exact rules or requirements** that must be followed.

---

## Why is a Standard Important?

- Ensures consistency across the organization.
- Defines minimum security requirements.
- Supports compliance.
- Reduces security risks.
- Makes policies easier to implement.

---

## Characteristics of a Standard

- Mandatory to follow.
- Specific and measurable.
- Supports a policy.
- Applies to all relevant users or systems.
- Ensures uniform implementation.

---

## Examples of Standards

- Password Standard (Minimum 12 characters)
- Encryption Standard (AES-256)
- Secure Configuration Standard
- Email Security Standard
- Data Retention Standard
- Network Security Standard
