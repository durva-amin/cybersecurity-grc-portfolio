- Explains **how** to perform a task.
- Step-by-step document.
- Detailed and specific.
- Supports policies.
- Mandatory to follow.


### Easy Trick to Remember

**Policy = WHAT** 📋

**Procedure = HOW** ⚙️

**Example:**

- **Policy:** Customer data must be backed up daily.
- **Procedure:** The exact steps to perform the daily backup.

This distinction is one of the most frequently tested concepts in GRC interviews.
