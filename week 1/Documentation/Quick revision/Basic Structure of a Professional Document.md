### 🔑 Easy Trick to Remember

**TPSVACR**

- **T** → Title
- **P** → Purpose
- **S** → Scope
- **V** → Version
- **A** → Author
- **C** → Content
- **R** → Review Date

Remember **"TPSVACR"** to quickly recall the structure during interviews.
