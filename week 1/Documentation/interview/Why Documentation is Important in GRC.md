## Interview Questions

### Q1. Why is documentation important in GRC?

**Answer:**  
Documentation supports governance, helps manage risks, demonstrates compliance, and provides evidence during audits.

### Q2. How does documentation help in risk management?

**Answer:**  
It records identified risks, their impact, likelihood, and the actions taken to reduce them.

### Q3. How does documentation support compliance?

**Answer:**  
It provides written evidence that the organization follows legal, regulatory, and security requirements.

### Q4. Why do auditors review documentation?

**Answer:**  
To verify that security controls and compliance requirements are properly implemented and maintained.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** During an ISO 27001 audit, the auditor asks for the company's Risk Assessment Report, but it doesn't exist. What could happen?

**Answer:**  
The organization may receive an audit finding or fail the audit because it cannot provide evidence that risks are identified and managed.




A common interview question is:

**"What role does documentation play in GRC?"**

**Answer:**  
Documentation connects all three pillars of GRC:

- **Governance:** Defines policies and responsibilities.
- **Risk:** Records risks and treatment plans.
- **Compliance:** Provides evidence for audits and regulatory requirements.

### Easy Trick to Remember

**GRC = 3D**

- **G**overnance → **D**efine rules 📋
- **R**isk → **D**ocument risks ⚠️
- **C**ompliance → **D**emonstrate evidence ✅

**One-line interview answer:**

> "In GRC, if it's not documented, it's difficult to prove it exists or is being followed." (This reflects a common auditing principle.)
