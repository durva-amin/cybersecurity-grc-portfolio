## Interview Questions

### Q1. Why are documents updated?

**Answer:**  
To keep them accurate, improve security, comply with new regulations, and reflect changes in business processes.

### Q2. When should a document be reviewed?

**Answer:**  
Regularly (usually annually) or whenever there are significant changes in business, technology, security threats, or regulations.

### Q3. Who is responsible for updating documents?

**Answer:**  
The document owner or the assigned department (such as the GRC or Information Security team), with approval from management when required.

### Q4. What could happen if documents are not updated?

**Answer:**  
Employees may follow outdated procedures, leading to security risks, audit findings, and compliance issues.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** A company still follows a security policy written five years ago, even though new cybersecurity threats and regulations have emerged. What is the problem?

**Answer:**  
The documentation is outdated and should be reviewed and updated to address current security risks and compliance requirements.





A common interview question is:

**"Is it enough to create a document once?"**

**Answer:**  
No. Documents must be reviewed and updated regularly to ensure they remain accurate, effective, and compliant with current business and regulatory requirements.

### 🔑 Easy Trick to Remember

**"4T Rule"**

Documents are updated because of changes in:

- **T**hreats ⚠️
- **T**echnology 💻
- **T**asks (Business Processes) 🏢
- **T**rules (Laws & Regulations) 📜
