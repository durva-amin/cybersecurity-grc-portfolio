## Interview Questions

### Q1. Why do companies write documents?

**Answer:**  
To ensure consistency, define responsibilities, reduce risks, support compliance, and provide clear guidance to employees.

### Q2. How do documents help employees?

**Answer:**  
They provide clear instructions on security rules, processes, and responsibilities.

### Q3. Why are documents important during an audit?

**Answer:**  
They provide evidence that the organization follows established security practices and compliance requirements.

### Q4. What can happen if a company does not maintain documentation?

**Answer:**  
Employees may follow different processes, leading to confusion, security incidents, audit failures, and compliance issues.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** Two employees follow different methods for handling customer data because there is no written process. What is the problem?

**Answer:**  
The company lacks proper documentation, causing inconsistent practices and increasing the risk of security incidents.


A common interview question is:

**"Can't a company just tell employees what to do instead of writing documents?"**

**Answer:**  
No. Verbal instructions can be forgotten or misunderstood. Written documentation ensures consistency, accountability, training, and provides evidence during audits.

### Easy Trick to Remember

**Documents = 6 C's**

- **C**onsistency
- **C**larity
- **C**ompliance
- **C**ommunication
- **C**ontrol
- **C**ontinuity

Remembering these six words helps you quickly explain why documentation is essential in GRC.
