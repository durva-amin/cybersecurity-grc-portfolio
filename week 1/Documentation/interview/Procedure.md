## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Procedure?

**Answer:**  
A procedure is a document that provides step-by-step instructions for performing a specific task.

### Q2. What does a procedure explain?

**Answer:**  
It explains **how** to perform a task or process.

### Q3. Why are procedures important?

**Answer:**  
They ensure consistency, reduce errors, and help employees perform tasks correctly.

### Q4. Is a procedure mandatory?

**Answer:**  
Yes. Employees must follow approved procedures for the tasks they perform.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** A document explains the exact steps employees must follow to report a phishing email. Is this a Policy or a Procedure?

**Answer:**  
**Procedure**, because it provides **step-by-step instructions**.




A common interview question is:

**Can a company have a policy without a procedure?**

**Answer:**  
Yes, but the policy may be difficult to implement. A policy defines **what** must be done, while a procedure explains **how** to do it. Together, they ensure consistent implementation.

### Easy Trick to Remember

**Policy = WHAT** 📋

**Procedure = HOW** ⚙️

**Example:**

- **Policy:** Customer data must be backed up daily.
- **Procedure:** The exact steps to perform the daily backup.

This distinction is one of the most frequently tested concepts in GRC interviews.
