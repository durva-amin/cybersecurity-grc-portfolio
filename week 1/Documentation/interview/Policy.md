## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Policy?

**Answer:**  
A policy is a high-level document that defines an organization's rules, objectives, and expectations.

### Q2. What does a policy define?

**Answer:**  
It defines **what must be done** to meet organizational or security requirements.

### Q3. Who approves a policy?

**Answer:**  
Senior management or top management.

### Q4. Is a policy mandatory?

**Answer:**  
Yes, policies are mandatory for everyone they apply to.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** A company states that "All confidential data must be encrypted." Is this a Policy or a Procedure?

**Answer:**  
**Policy**, because it states **what must be done** without explaining the steps.



A very common interview question is:

**Policy vs Procedure**

**Policy:** Tells **what** must be done.

**Procedure:** Explains **how** to do it.

### Easy Trick to Remember

**Policy = WHAT** 📋

**Procedure = HOW** ⚙️

**Example:**

- **Policy:** Employees must use MFA.
- **Procedure:** Steps to enable MFA on a company account.

This is one of the most frequently asked GRC interview questions, so remember **Policy = WHAT, Procedure = HOW**.
