## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Standard?

**Answer:**  
A standard is a document that defines specific mandatory requirements that must be followed.

### Q2. Why are standards important?

**Answer:**  
They ensure consistency, improve security, and define minimum requirements for implementing policies.

### Q3. Is a standard mandatory?

**Answer:**  
Yes. Standards are mandatory and must be followed.

### Q4. Give an example of a standard.

**Answer:**  
A password must contain at least 12 characters, one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character.

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** A company requires all laptops to use **AES-256 encryption**. Is this a Policy, Procedure, or Standard?

**Answer:**  
**Standard**, because it specifies the exact security requirement (AES-256).




A common interview question is:

**Policy vs Standard**

**Policy:** Tells **what** must be done.

**Standard:** Defines the **exact requirements** that must be followed.

### Easy Trick to Remember

- **Policy = WHAT** 📋
- **Standard = EXACT REQUIREMENTS** 📏

**Example:**

- **Policy:** Use strong passwords.
- **Standard:** Passwords must be at least 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character.
