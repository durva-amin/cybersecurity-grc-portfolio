## Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Guideline?

**Answer:**  
A guideline is a document that provides recommended best practices to help employees perform tasks securely and efficiently.

### Q2. Is a guideline mandatory?

**Answer:**  
Generally, no. It is a recommendation unless the organization specifically makes it mandatory.

### Q3. Why are guidelines important?

**Answer:**  
They help employees follow best practices and make informed decisions while allowing flexibility.

### Q4. Give one example of a guideline.

**Answer:**  
Using a password manager to create and store strong passwords.

---

## Scenario-Based Question

**Q.** A company recommends that employees use a password manager, but it is not compulsory. Is this a Policy, Standard, Procedure, or Guideline?

**Answer:**  
**Guideline**, because it is a recommendation rather than a mandatory requirement.



A very common interview question is:

**Policy vs Procedure vs Standard vs Guideline**

|Document|Purpose|
|---|---|
|**Policy**|**What** must be done|
|**Procedure**|**How** to do it|
|**Standard**|**Exact mandatory requirements**|
|**Guideline**|**Recommended best practices**|

### Easy Trick to Remember

- **Policy = WHAT** 📋
- **Procedure = HOW** ⚙️
- **Standard = EXACT RULES** 📏
- **Guideline = SUGGESTION** 💡
