### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a detective control?**  
**Answer:** A detective control is designed to identify or detect security incidents, suspicious activity, or weaknesses.

**Q2. Give an example of a detective control.**  
**Answer:** An intrusion detection system (IDS) is a detective control because it monitors activity and alerts when potentially malicious activity is detected.

**Q3. What is the difference between preventive and detective controls?**  
**Answer:** Preventive controls aim to **stop an incident before it occurs**, while detective controls aim to **identify an incident or suspicious activity**.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. A company monitors login logs and alerts the security team when multiple failed login attempts occur. What type of control is this?**

**Answer:** It is a **detective control** because it identifies suspicious authentication activity.
