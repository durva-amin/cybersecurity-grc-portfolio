### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is control evidence?**  
**Answer:** Control evidence is information that demonstrates that a security control has been implemented or operated as required.

**Q2. Why is control evidence important?**  
**Answer:** It provides support that a control is actually operating and helps auditors or reviewers verify the control.

**Q3. What can be used as control evidence?**  
**Answer:** Depending on the control, evidence can include screenshots, configuration records, logs, reports, review records, tickets, or other relevant documentation.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An organization claims that quarterly access reviews are performed. What could an auditor request to verify this?**

**Answer:** The auditor could request completed access review records or reports showing the review date, users reviewed, and responsible reviewer.
