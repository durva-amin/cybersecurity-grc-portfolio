### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a preventive control?**  
**Answer:** A preventive control is designed to stop or reduce the likelihood of a security incident before it occurs.

**Q2. Give an example of a preventive control.**  
**Answer:** MFA is a preventive control because it helps prevent unauthorized access to accounts.

**Q3. What is the difference between preventive and detective controls?**  
**Answer:** Preventive controls aim to **stop an incident from occurring**, while detective controls aim to **identify an incident or issue after or while it occurs**.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An organization requires MFA for all employees accessing company systems. What type of control is MFA?**

**Answer:** MFA is a **preventive control** because it helps prevent unauthorized access.
