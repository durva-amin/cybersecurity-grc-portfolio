### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is control evidence?**  
**Answer:** Control evidence is information that demonstrates the implementation or operation of a security control.

**Q2. Why is control evidence important?**  
**Answer:** It helps auditors and organizations verify that controls are actually implemented and operating as required.

**Q3. Give examples of control evidence.**  
**Answer:** Screenshots, system logs, configuration records, access review reports, tickets, policies, and other relevant records.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An organization says that quarterly access reviews are performed. What evidence could an auditor request?**

**Answer:** The auditor could request completed access review records showing the review date, users reviewed, reviewer, and any resulting access changes.
