### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is an evidence owner?**  
**Answer:** An evidence owner is the person or team responsible for providing or maintaining the evidence associated with a security control.

**Q2. Is the evidence owner always the same as the control owner?**  
**Answer:** No. The control owner is accountable for the control, while the evidence owner may be responsible for providing or maintaining the supporting evidence.

**Q3. Why is an evidence owner important?**  
**Answer:** It establishes clear responsibility for collecting, maintaining, and providing evidence when required.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. During an audit, an access review report is required, but nobody knows who should provide it. What should have been documented?**

**Answer:** An **Evidence Owner** should have been assigned to clearly identify who is responsible for providing the report.
