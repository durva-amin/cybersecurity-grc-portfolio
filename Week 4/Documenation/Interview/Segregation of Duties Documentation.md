### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is Segregation of Duties?**  
**Answer:** It is a control principle that separates critical responsibilities among different individuals to reduce the risk of fraud, errors, or misuse.

**Q2. Why is SoD important?**  
**Answer:** It prevents excessive control from being concentrated with one person and provides checks and balances.

**Q3. What is an example of an SoD conflict?**  
**Answer:** The same employee being able to both **create and approve** a financial transaction.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An employee can create a new vendor, approve the vendor, and process payments to that vendor. What is the concern?**

**Answer:** There is a **Segregation of Duties conflict** because one person has control over multiple critical stages of the process.
