### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is control implementation?**  
**Answer:** It describes how a security control is deployed and operated within an organization.

**Q2. Why is control implementation important?**  
**Answer:** It shows how the documented control is actually put into practice and helps verify that the control is operating as intended.

**Q3. What can be included when documenting implementation?**  
**Answer:** The technology, process, configuration, responsible team, or method used to implement the control.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An organization has a documented MFA requirement, but you want to know how it is actually enforced. What should you document?**

**Answer:** The **control implementation**, such as how MFA is configured and enforced through the organization's authentication system.
