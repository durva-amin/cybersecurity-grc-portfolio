### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is an evidence source?**  
**Answer:** It identifies the system, application, document, or other source from which control evidence was obtained.

**Q2. Why is the evidence source important?**  
**Answer:** It helps establish where the evidence originated and makes the evidence easier to verify and trace.

**Q3. Give examples of evidence sources.**  
**Answer:** Security tools, system configuration pages, audit logs, access review reports, ticketing systems, policies, or other organizational records.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An auditor receives an access review report but wants to know where the report was generated. What information should be documented?**

**Answer:** The **Evidence Source**, such as the organization's identity management system or access management platform.
