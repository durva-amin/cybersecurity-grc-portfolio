### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is evidence retention?**  
**Answer:** Evidence retention defines how long control evidence should be kept and maintained.

**Q2. Why is evidence retention important?**  
**Answer:** It ensures that required evidence remains available for audits, compliance reviews, investigations, and other legitimate business needs.

**Q3. What determines how long evidence should be retained?**  
**Answer:** Retention requirements may depend on organizational policies, legal or regulatory requirements, contractual obligations, and the nature of the evidence.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An auditor requests evidence of access reviews performed six months ago, but the organization deleted the records after one month. What documentation practice was missing or inadequate?**

**Answer:** **Evidence retention** was not properly implemented, because the records were not retained for the required period.
