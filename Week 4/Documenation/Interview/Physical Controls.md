### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a physical control?**  
**Answer:** A physical control is a safeguard used to protect physical facilities, equipment, people, and information from physical threats.

**Q2. Give an example of a physical control.**  
**Answer:** An access card system is a physical control because it restricts physical entry to authorized personnel.

**Q3. What is the difference between physical and technical controls?**  
**Answer:** Physical controls protect against **physical threats**, while technical controls use **technology or technical mechanisms** to protect systems and information.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. A company requires employees to use access cards to enter its server room. What type of control is this?**

**Answer:** It is a **physical control** because it restricts physical access to the server room.
