### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is an administrative control?**  
**Answer:** An administrative control is a policy, procedure, rule, or management practice used to manage security risks.

**Q2. Give an example of an administrative control.**  
**Answer:** A password policy is an administrative control because it establishes rules for how employees should create and manage passwords.

**Q3. What is the difference between administrative and technical controls?**  
**Answer:** Administrative controls focus mainly on **policies, processes, and people**, while technical controls use **technology or systems** to enforce security.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An organization creates a policy requiring employees to use MFA and provides security awareness training explaining why MFA is important. What type of control is the policy and training?**

**Answer:** They are **administrative controls** because they establish organizational requirements and educate users about security practices.
