### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a control owner?**  
**Answer:** A control owner is the person or team responsible for managing and maintaining a security control.

**Q2. Why is control ownership important?**  
**Answer:** It establishes clear accountability for the control and ensures someone is responsible for its operation and maintenance.

**Q3. Can a control have more than one person involved?**  
**Answer:** Yes. Different people or teams may support a control, but there should be clear accountability for who owns it.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. A company has an access review control, but nobody is assigned responsibility for performing the review. What is missing?**

**Answer:** A clearly defined **Control Owner** is missing.
