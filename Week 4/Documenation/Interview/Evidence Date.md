### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is an evidence date?**  
**Answer:** It records when the control evidence was created, collected, generated, or captured.

**Q2. Why is the evidence date important?**  
**Answer:** It helps establish the timeframe of the evidence and allows reviewers to determine whether it is relevant to the period being assessed.

**Q3. Is the evidence date always the same as the control activity date?**  
**Answer:** Not necessarily. The evidence may be generated or collected at a different time from when the control activity occurred.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An auditor is reviewing quarterly access reviews and receives a report without a date. Why could this be a problem?**

**Answer:** The auditor may not be able to determine **when the review was performed** or whether the evidence relates to the required review period.
