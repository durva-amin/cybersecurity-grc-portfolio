### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is need-to-know?**  
**Answer:** Need-to-know means providing access to information only when it is necessary for a person's authorized job responsibilities.

**Q2. What is the difference between least privilege and need-to-know?**  
**Answer:** Least privilege limits the **level of access or permissions**, while need-to-know limits **which information or resources a person needs to access**.

**Q3. Why is need-to-know important?**  
**Answer:** It reduces unnecessary exposure of sensitive information and helps limit the impact of unauthorized access.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An employee has access to confidential customer records even though their job does not require them to view those records. What principle is being violated?**

**Answer:** The **need-to-know principle** is being violated because the employee does not require that information for their job responsibilities.
