### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a compensating control?**  
**Answer:** It is an alternative control used to address or reduce a security risk when the preferred control cannot be implemented as required.

**Q2. Why are compensating controls used?**  
**Answer:** They help manage security risk when a standard or preferred control cannot be implemented because of technical, operational, or other constraints.

**Q3. Is a compensating control simply a replacement for any missing control?**  
**Answer:** No. It should be appropriate for the risk and provide an acceptable level of protection.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. A legacy application cannot support MFA. What could the organization do instead of leaving the application without additional protection?**

**Answer:** Implement appropriate compensating controls, such as restricting network access, limiting authorized users, and increasing monitoring.
