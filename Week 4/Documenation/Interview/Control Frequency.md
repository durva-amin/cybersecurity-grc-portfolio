### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is control frequency?**  
**Answer:** It defines how often a security control is performed, monitored, or reviewed.

**Q2. Why is control frequency important?**  
**Answer:** It ensures that controls are performed consistently and that important security activities are not overlooked.

**Q3. Does every security control have the same frequency?**  
**Answer:** No. Frequency depends on the purpose of the control, the associated risk, and organizational requirements.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An organization reviews employee access every three months. What is the control frequency?**

**Answer:** **Quarterly.**
