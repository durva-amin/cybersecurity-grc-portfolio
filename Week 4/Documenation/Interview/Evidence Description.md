### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is an evidence description?**  
**Answer:** It is a brief explanation of what the evidence demonstrates and how it supports the related security control.

**Q2. Why is an evidence description important?**  
**Answer:** It helps reviewers quickly understand the relevance of the evidence and its connection to the control.

**Q3. What makes a good evidence description?**  
**Answer:** It should be clear, specific, factual, and directly related to the control.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An auditor receives a screenshot showing that MFA is enabled but does not know what part of the screenshot is relevant. What could help?**

**Answer:** A clear **evidence description** explaining what the screenshot demonstrates and how it supports the MFA control.
