### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is least privilege?**  
**Answer:** Least privilege means providing only the minimum access necessary for a user or system to perform its required function.

**Q2. Why is least privilege important?**  
**Answer:** It reduces the risk of unauthorized access, accidental changes, and the potential impact if an account is compromised.

**Q3. What should least privilege documentation demonstrate?**  
**Answer:** It should demonstrate that access is limited according to job responsibilities and business requirements.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An employee only needs to view customer records but has permission to modify and delete them. What should be done?**

**Answer:** Review the employee's access and reduce it to the minimum required level, such as **read-only access**.
