### Interview Questions

**Q1. Why should security controls be documented?**  
**Answer:** To clearly define what the control does, who owns it, how it is implemented, and what evidence demonstrates its operation.

**Q2. What information can be included when documenting a security control?**  
**Answer:** Control objective, description, owner, type, frequency, implementation, and evidence.

**Q3. Why is control ownership important?**  
**Answer:** It establishes accountability for implementing, maintaining, and reviewing the control.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An organization has an MFA control but has not documented who is responsible for it or how it is monitored. What is missing?**

**Answer:** Important control documentation such as the **Control Owner, Control Implementation, and Control Frequency** is missing.
