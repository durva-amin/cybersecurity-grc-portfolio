### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is a control objective?**  
**Answer:** A control objective defines the security goal that a control is intended to achieve.

**Q2. What is the difference between a control objective and a control description?**  
**Answer:** The **objective explains what the control should achieve**, while the **description explains what the control does**.

**Q3. Why is a control objective important?**  
**Answer:** It helps determine whether the control is appropriate for addressing a particular security risk.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. An organization implements MFA to reduce unauthorized account access. What would be the control objective?**

**Answer:** To prevent or reduce unauthorized access by requiring additional authentication verification.
