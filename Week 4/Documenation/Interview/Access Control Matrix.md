### Interview Questions

**Q1. What is an Access Control Matrix?**  
**Answer:** It is a table that maps users or roles to resources and defines the access permissions they have.

**Q2. Why is an Access Control Matrix useful?**  
**Answer:** It provides a clear view of access permissions and helps identify unnecessary or excessive access.

**Q3. What can an Access Control Matrix help identify?**  
**Answer:** It can help identify unauthorized access, excessive privileges, missing permissions, and potential access conflicts.

### Scenario-Based Question

**Q. During an access review, you discover that an HR employee has access to financial records even though their role does not require it. How can an Access Control Matrix help?**

**Answer:** The matrix can show that the HR role has unnecessary access to financial records, allowing the organization to review and remove that access.
