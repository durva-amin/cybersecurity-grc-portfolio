### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Least Privilege = Minimum necessary access**

Ask:

> **"Does this user really need this level of access to perform their job?"**

### Quick Revision

- Give users **only necessary access**.
- Access should match **job responsibilities**.
- Reduces unauthorized access and potential damage.
- Review and remove **unnecessary privileges**.
- Example: **Read-only instead of modify/delete access when only viewing is required.**
