### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Detective = Find it.**

Preventive asks: **"How can we stop it?"**  
Detective asks: **"Did something happen?"**

### Quick Revision

- Detects security incidents or suspicious activity.
- Helps identify problems **during or after an event**.
- Examples: **IDS, audit logs, security monitoring, log monitoring**.
- Think: **"How can we detect it?"**
