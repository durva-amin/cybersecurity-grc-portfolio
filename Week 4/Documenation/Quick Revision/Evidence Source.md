### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Evidence = What proves it?**  
**Evidence Description = What does it show?**  
**Evidence Source = Where did it come from?**

### Quick Revision

- Identifies **where evidence originated**.
- Helps with **traceability and verification**.
- Can be a **system, application, document, report, or organizational record**.
- Example: **Access review report → Identity Management System**.
