### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Control Owner = Who is responsible for the control?**

Don't confuse the **control owner** with every person who performs an activity related to the control. The owner has overall accountability.

### Quick Revision

- Control Owner = **Person/team responsible for a control**.
- Provides **accountability**.
- Helps ensure the control is implemented and maintained.
- Example: **IT Security Team owns the MFA control.**
