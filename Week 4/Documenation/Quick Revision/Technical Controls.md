### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Technical = Technology**

If the control is implemented through a **system, software, hardware, or technical mechanism**, it can be classified as a technical control.

### Quick Revision

- Uses **technology to manage security risks**.
- Protects systems, networks, applications, and information.
- Examples: **Firewall, MFA, encryption, antivirus, IDS**.
- Think: **"What technology is enforcing the security?"**
