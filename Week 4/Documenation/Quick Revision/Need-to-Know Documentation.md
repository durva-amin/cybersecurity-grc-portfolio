### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Least Privilege = How much access do you need?**  
**Need-to-Know = What information do you need?**

### Quick Revision

- Access to information should be based on **business need**.
- Prevents unnecessary exposure of sensitive information.
- Access should be given only to **authorized personnel who require it**.
- Example: **HR can access salary records when required for their responsibilities.**
