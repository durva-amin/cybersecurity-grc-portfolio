### Interview Tip ⭐

Remember:

**Physical = Protect the physical environment**

Think about **doors, buildings, equipment, people, and physical access**.

### Quick Revision

- Protects **facilities, equipment, people, and physical information**.
- Helps prevent unauthorized physical access, theft, or damage.
- Examples: **locks, CCTV, security guards, access cards, biometrics**.
- Think: **"Who can physically enter or access it?"**
