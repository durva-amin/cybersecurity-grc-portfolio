### Definition

**Control Frequency** specifies **how often a security control is performed, checked, or reviewed**.

It helps an organization determine when the control needs to operate or be assessed.

### Common Frequencies

- **Continuous:** Control operates continuously.
- **Daily:** Performed every day.
- **Weekly:** Performed once a week.
- **Monthly:** Performed once a month.
- **Quarterly:** Performed once every three months.
- **Annually:** Performed once a year.
- **Event-based:** Performed when a specific event occurs.
