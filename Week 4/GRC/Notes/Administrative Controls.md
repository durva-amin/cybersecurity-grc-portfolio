## Definition

**Administrative Controls** are security measures based on **policies, procedures, rules, processes, and management practices** used to manage how people protect an organization's information and systems.

**Simple Definition:**

> Administrative controls tell people **what they should do, what they are allowed to do, and how security should be managed**.

---

## Examples of Administrative Controls

Common examples include:

- Security policies
- Security procedures
- Employee security training
- Background checks
- Access control policies
- Risk assessments
- Incident response procedures
- Vendor management procedures
- Employee onboarding and offboarding procedures

---

## Why Are Administrative Controls Important?

Technology alone cannot protect an organization.

Administrative controls help:

- Define security responsibilities.
- Establish rules employees must follow.
- Reduce human-related security risks.
- Ensure security processes are followed consistently.
- Support compliance requirements.


## Administrative vs Technical vs Physical

|Control|Focus|Example|
|---|---|---|
|**Administrative**|People & processes|Security policy|
|**Technical**|Technology|Firewall|
|**Physical**|Physical protection|Door lock|
