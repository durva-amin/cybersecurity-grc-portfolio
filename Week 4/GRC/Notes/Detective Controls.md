## Definition

**Detective Controls** are security controls designed to **identify or discover security incidents, suspicious activities, errors, or policy violations after or while they occur**.

**Simple Definition:**

> Detective controls help us **find out that something wrong has happened or is happening**.

---

## What Do Detective Controls Do?

Detective controls help organizations:

- Detect suspicious activity
- Identify security incidents
- Discover unauthorized access
- Identify policy violations
- Generate alerts
- Provide evidence for investigation

They mainly **detect** problems rather than directly preventing them.

---

## Examples of Detective Controls

Common examples include:

- Intrusion Detection System (**IDS**)
- Security monitoring
- Log monitoring
- SIEM alerts
- CCTV monitoring
- Security audits
- File integrity monitoring
- Reviewing failed login attempts


## Detective vs Preventive

|Control|Purpose|Example|
|---|---|---|
|**Preventive**|Stop something from happening|Firewall blocking traffic|
|**Detective**|Identify something happening/happened|IDS generating alert|

> **Preventive = Stop**  
> **Detective = Find**
