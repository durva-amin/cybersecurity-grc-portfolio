## Definition

**Physical Controls** are security measures used to **physically protect people, buildings, equipment, and information systems from unauthorized access, theft, damage, or other physical threats**.

**Simple Definition:**

> Physical controls protect an organization's **physical locations and assets**.

---

## Examples of Physical Controls

Common examples include:

- Door locks
- Security guards
- CCTV cameras
- Biometric door access
- Access cards/badges
- Fences and gates
- Server-room locks
- Security barriers
- Fire detection/suppression systems
- Visitor access controls

---

## What Do Physical Controls Do?

Physical controls help:

- Prevent unauthorized people from entering restricted areas.
- Protect servers and devices from theft.
- Monitor physical locations.
- Protect facilities from physical damage.
- Restrict access to sensitive areas such as server rooms
