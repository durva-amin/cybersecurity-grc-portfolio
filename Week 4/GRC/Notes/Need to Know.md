## Definition

**Need to Know** is a security principle where a person is given access to **specific information only when that information is necessary to perform their job or task**.

**Simple Definition:**

> You can see information **only if you need it for your work**.

---

## Why Is Need to Know Important?

It helps:

- Protect confidential information
- Prevent unnecessary data exposure
- Reduce insider-threat risk
- Limit unauthorized disclosure
- Protect sensitive business and personal data

---

## How Does It Work?

Basic idea:

**What is the person's job? → What information do they need? → Give access only to that information**

Just because an employee works for the company does **not** mean they should have access to all company information.
