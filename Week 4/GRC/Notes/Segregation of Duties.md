## Definition

**Segregation of Duties (SoD)** is a security principle where **critical tasks and responsibilities are divided among multiple people** so that one person does not have complete control over an important process.

**Simple Definition:**

> Don't give **one person complete control** over a critical process.

---

## Why Is Segregation of Duties Important?

SoD helps:

- Prevent fraud
- Reduce human errors
- Prevent misuse of authority
- Detect unauthorized activities
- Reduce insider threats
- Create accountability

The main idea is:

> **One person should not be able to perform and approve an entire sensitive transaction alone.**

---

## How Does It Work?

A critical process is divided into different responsibilities.

For example:

**Person A → Creates request**

**Person B → Approves request**

**Person C → Processes/reviews transaction**

This reduces the chance that one person can misuse the process without being noticed.
