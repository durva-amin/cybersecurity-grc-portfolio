## Definition

**Compensating Controls** are **alternative security controls used when the original or required control cannot be implemented**, while still reducing the related risk.

**Simple Definition:**

> If the main security control **cannot be used**, we use another control to provide **similar protection**.

---

## Why Are Compensating Controls Used?

A company may not be able to implement the preferred control because of:

- Technical limitations
- Legacy systems
- Cost constraints
- Business requirements
- Temporary operational limitations

Instead of leaving the risk completely unaddressed, the organization implements an **alternative control**.
