## Definition

**Least Privilege** is a security principle where users, applications, and systems are given **only the minimum permissions necessary to perform their required tasks**.

**Simple Definition:**

> Give a person **only the access they need — nothing extra**.

---

## Why Is Least Privilege Important?

Least Privilege helps:

- Prevent unauthorized access
- Reduce misuse of permissions
- Limit damage if an account is compromised
- Reduce insider-threat risk
- Protect sensitive information
- Reduce the attack surface

---

## How Does It Work?

Basic idea:

**Identify job/task → Determine required access → Give minimum permissions → Review access regularly → Remove unnecessary access**

For example:

An HR employee needs access to employee records but does **not** need access to the company's financial systems.

So:

**HR system → Access ✅**

**Finance system → No access ❌**
