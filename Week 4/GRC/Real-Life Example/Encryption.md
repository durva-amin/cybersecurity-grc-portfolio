Suppose a company laptop containing confidential customer information is stolen.

If the drive is properly encrypted, the thief may possess the physical device but cannot easily read the stored information without the required credentials/key.

This reduces the risk of data exposure.

# Encryption vs Encoding vs Hashing

Only remember the basic difference for now:

|Method|Main Purpose|Reversible?|
|---|---|---|
|**Encryption**|Confidentiality|✅ Yes, with appropriate key|
|**Hashing**|Integrity/verification|❌ Designed to be one-way|
|**Encoding**|Data representation/compatibility|✅ Yes, no secret key needed|

You'll study **Hashing** separately.
