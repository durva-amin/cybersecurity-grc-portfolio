# Real-Life Example

An employee receives:

```
URGENT!

Your company account will be disabled.
Click here immediately and enter your password.
```

Because of security awareness training, the employee:

1. Recognizes the suspicious message.
2. Does not click the link.
3. Reports the email to the security team.

The training helped reduce the likelihood of a successful phishing attack.

---

# What Type of Control Is Security Awareness?

Security Awareness Training is generally an:

> **Administrative Control**

It can also perform a **Preventive function** because it helps prevent employees from making security mistakes.

Remember:

> **Administrative = How it is implemented**

> **Preventive = What it is intended to do**
