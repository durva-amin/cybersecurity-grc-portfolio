## Real-Life Example

Suppose a company needs to pay a vendor ₹5 lakh.

❌ **Without SoD:**

One employee can:

**Create vendor → Create payment → Approve payment → Send money**

This creates a high fraud risk.

✅ **With SoD:**

**Employee A → Creates payment request**

**Manager B → Approves payment**

**Finance Employee C → Processes payment**

Now one person does not control the complete process.

---

## SoD in Cybersecurity

Suppose an IT administrator requests highly privileged access.

A separate manager or authorized person should approve the request rather than allowing the administrator to **request and approve their own access**.

This provides separation between:

**Request → Approval → Implementation**
