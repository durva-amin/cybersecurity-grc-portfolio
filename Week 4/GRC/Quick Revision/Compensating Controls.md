# Quick Revision

- **Compensating = Alternative Control**
- Used when the preferred/required control **cannot be implemented**
- Should provide meaningful **risk reduction**
- Common reasons:
    - Legacy technology
    - Technical limitations
    - Business constraints
    - Cost constraints
- Important in GRC:
    - Assess
    - Document
    - Approve
    - Monitor
    - Review

### ⭐ Easy Trick

> **Compensating = Plan B 🔄**

**Control A cannot be implemented → Use suitable Control B to reduce the same risk.**
