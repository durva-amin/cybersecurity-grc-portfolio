# Quick Revision

- **Administrative Controls = People + Policies + Processes**
- Examples:
    - Policies
    - Procedures
    - Training
    - Risk assessments
    - Background checks
    - Onboarding/offboarding
- Helps establish **security rules and responsibilities**.
- Very important in **GRC**.

### ⭐ Easy Trick

Remember:

**Administrative → People & Rules 👥📋**

**Technical → Technology 💻**

**Physical → Physical protection 🔐**

> **Policy says what should happen → Administrative**  
> **Technology enforces it → Technical**
