# Quick Revision

- **Physical Controls = Physical protection**
- Protect:
    - People
    - Buildings
    - Equipment
    - Server rooms
    - Other physical assets
- Examples:
    - Locks
    - CCTV
    - Guards
    - Access cards
    - Fences
    - Biometric entry

### ⭐ Easy Trick

> **Administrative → Rules 📋**  
> **Technical → Technology 💻**  
> **Physical → Things you can physically protect 🚪🔐**

**Server room + locked door + CCTV + guard = Physical Security**
