# Quick Revision

- **Need to Know = Only necessary information**
- Protects confidential/sensitive information
- Reduces unnecessary data exposure
- Access should be based on job requirements

### ⭐ Easy Trick

> **Least Privilege → What can I DO? 🔑**  
> **Need to Know → What do I need to KNOW? 📄**

**Don't need the information for your job? → Don't get access.**
