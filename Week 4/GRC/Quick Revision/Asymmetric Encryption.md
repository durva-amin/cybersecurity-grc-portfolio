# Quick Revision

- **Asymmetric = TWO keys**
- 🔓 **Public Key → Can be shared**
- 🔐 **Private Key → Must remain secret**
- Examples → **RSA, ECC**
- Generally slower than symmetric encryption
- Supports:
    - Secure key establishment
    - Encryption
    - Digital signatures

### ⭐ Easy Trick

> **Symmetric → SAME key 🔑**  
> **Asymmetric → A PAIR of keys 🔑🔑**

And remember:

**Public = Share 🌍**

**Private = Secret 🤫**
