# Quick Revision

- **Least Privilege = Minimum required access**
- Give only necessary:
    - Permissions
    - Access
    - Privileges
- Applies to:
    - Users
    - Applications
    - Services
    - Systems
- Reduces:
    - Unauthorized access
    - Insider risk
    - Impact of compromised accounts
    - Attack surface

### ⭐ Easy Trick

> **Least Privilege = Need 5 permissions? Give 5, not 10.**

**Minimum Access → Minimum Exposure → Lower Risk 🔐**
