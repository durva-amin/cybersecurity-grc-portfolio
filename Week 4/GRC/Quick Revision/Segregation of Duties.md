# Quick Revision

- **SoD = Divide critical responsibilities**
- One person should not control the entire sensitive process.
- Helps prevent:
    - Fraud
    - Errors
    - Misuse
    - Insider threats
- Example:
    - Person A → Request
    - Person B → Approve
- **SoD conflict = One person has incompatible responsibilities**

### ⭐ Easy Trick

> **SoD = Separate the Duties 👥**

Remember:

**One person creates + same person approves = ❌**

**One person creates + another approves = ✅**
