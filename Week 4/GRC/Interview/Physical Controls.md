# Interview Questions

### Q1. What are Physical Controls?

**Answer:**  
Physical controls are security measures designed to protect physical locations, people, equipment, and other assets from unauthorized access, theft, or damage.

### Q2. Give examples of Physical Controls.

**Answer:**  
Door locks, security guards, CCTV cameras, access cards, fences, biometric entry systems, and server-room locks.

### Q3. Is CCTV a Physical Control?

**Answer:**  
Yes. CCTV is commonly classified as a physical security control because it monitors physical locations.

### Q4. Is a firewall a Physical Control?

**Answer:**  
No. A firewall is a **Technical Control**.

### Q5. Why are Physical Controls important?

**Answer:**  
Because strong cybersecurity cannot protect a system if an unauthorized person can physically access or steal the equipment.

### Q6. Is an access-card system a Physical Control or Technical Control?

**Answer:**  
It can involve both. The technology is technical, but when it is used to restrict entry to a physical location, it is commonly categorized as a **physical access control**.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company discovers that anyone can enter its server room. What controls would you recommend?

**Answer:**  
I would recommend physical controls such as:

- Locked doors
- Access cards or biometric access
- CCTV monitoring
- Restricting entry to authorized employees

This reduces the risk of unauthorized physical access.
