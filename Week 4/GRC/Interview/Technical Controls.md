# Interview Questions

### Q1. What are Technical Controls?

**Answer:**  
Technical controls are security measures implemented through hardware or software to protect systems, networks, applications, and data.

### Q2. Give examples of Technical Controls.

**Answer:**  
Firewalls, antivirus, MFA, encryption, IDS/IPS, and technical access controls.

### Q3. Is a firewall a Technical Control?

**Answer:**  
Yes. A firewall uses technology to monitor and control network traffic based on configured rules.

### Q4. Is Security Awareness Training a Technical Control?

**Answer:**  
No. It is an **Administrative Control** because it focuses on people and security practices.

### Q5. Is MFA a Technical Control?

**Answer:**  
Yes. MFA is generally implemented through technology to strengthen authentication.

### Q6. Why are Technical Controls important?

**Answer:**  
They help prevent, detect, and respond to security threats and protect systems and information from unauthorized access.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company wants to prevent unauthorized users from accessing employee accounts even if passwords are stolen. It enables MFA. What type of control is this?

**Answer:**  
It is a **Technical Control** because a technical mechanism is being used to strengthen authentication.
