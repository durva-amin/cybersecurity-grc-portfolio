# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Security Awareness?

**Answer:**  
Security awareness is educating employees about security threats, organizational requirements, and secure practices so they can protect company information and systems.

### Q2. Why is Security Awareness important?

**Answer:**  
Because employees can be targeted through phishing, social engineering, and other attacks. Training helps them recognize threats and respond appropriately.

### Q3. What topics can Security Awareness Training cover?

**Answer:**  
Phishing, password security, social engineering, data protection, incident reporting, and physical security.

### Q4. Is Security Awareness an Administrative or Technical Control?

**Answer:**  
It is generally an **Administrative Control**.

### Q5. Can Security Awareness also be Preventive?

**Answer:**  
Yes. It can perform a preventive function by reducing the likelihood of employees falling for attacks or making security mistakes.

### Q6. Should Security Awareness Training happen only once?

**Answer:**  
No. Organizations should provide awareness activities periodically and update them based on changing threats and business requirements.

### Q7. How can an organization measure Security Awareness effectiveness?

**Answer:**  
It can use training completion rates, quizzes, phishing simulations, reporting rates, and other relevant security metrics.

### Q8. What should an employee do after receiving a suspicious email?

**Answer:**  
They should avoid interacting with suspicious links or attachments and report the message according to the organization's security procedures.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. During an audit, you discover that many employees are clicking simulated phishing emails. What would you recommend?

**Answer:**  
I would recommend improving the **Security Awareness Program**, including targeted phishing training, periodic simulations, clear reporting procedures, and follow-up training for employees who need additional support.

The organization should also measure whether employee performance improves over time.
