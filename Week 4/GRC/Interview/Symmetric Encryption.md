# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Symmetric Encryption?

**Answer:**  
Symmetric encryption uses the same secret key to encrypt and decrypt data.

### Q2. Give an example of a Symmetric Encryption algorithm.

**Answer:**  
**AES — Advanced Encryption Standard.**

### Q3. What is the main advantage of Symmetric Encryption?

**Answer:**  
It is generally fast and efficient, making it suitable for encrypting large amounts of data.

### Q4. What is the main challenge of Symmetric Encryption?

**Answer:**  
Securely distributing and managing the shared secret key.

### Q5. What happens if the secret key is compromised?

**Answer:**  
An attacker who obtains the key may be able to decrypt data protected with that key, so the affected key and data must be handled according to the organization's key-management and incident-response procedures.

### Q6. Is AES Symmetric or Asymmetric?

**Answer:**  
**Symmetric.**

### Q7. Is DES recommended today?

**Answer:**  
No. DES is considered insecure for modern use. Modern systems commonly use AES instead.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company needs to encrypt a very large amount of stored data efficiently. Which type of encryption would commonly be suitable?

**Answer:**  
**Symmetric Encryption**, such as **AES**, because it is fast and efficient for bulk data encryption.

The encryption keys must also be securely managed.
