# Interview Questions

### Q1. What are Administrative Controls?

**Answer:**  
Administrative controls are security measures based on policies, procedures, processes, and management practices used to manage people and organizational security.

### Q2. Give examples of Administrative Controls.

**Answer:**  
Security policies, employee training, risk assessments, background checks, incident response procedures, and access control policies.

### Q3. Is a Password Policy an Administrative Control?

**Answer:**  
Yes. A password policy establishes organizational rules for creating and managing passwords.

### Q4. Is a Firewall an Administrative Control?

**Answer:**  
No. A firewall is generally a **technical control** because it uses technology to control network traffic.

### Q5. Is Security Awareness Training an Administrative Control?

**Answer:**  
Yes. It is an administrative control because it helps establish and reinforce secure employee behavior.

### Q6. Why are Administrative Controls important in GRC?

**Answer:**  
They help organizations define responsibilities, establish security requirements, manage risks, and demonstrate that security processes and governance are in place.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. Employees are repeatedly clicking phishing links. The company introduces mandatory annual phishing awareness training. What type of control is this?

**Answer:**  
An **Administrative Control**, because security awareness training focuses on people, procedures, and organizational security practices.
