# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Segregation of Duties?

**Answer:**  
Segregation of Duties is the practice of dividing critical responsibilities among multiple people so that one person does not have complete control over a sensitive process.

### Q2. Why is SoD important?

**Answer:**  
It reduces the risk of fraud, errors, misuse of privileges, and unauthorized activities.

### Q3. Give an example of SoD.

**Answer:**  
One employee creates a payment request and another employee approves it.

### Q4. What can happen if SoD is not implemented?

**Answer:**  
One person may have excessive control, increasing the risk of fraud, mistakes, or misuse going undetected.

### Q5. How is SoD different from Least Privilege?

**Answer:**  
**Least Privilege** limits how much access a person receives, while **Segregation of Duties** divides critical responsibilities among different people.

### Q6. What is an SoD conflict?

**Answer:**  
An **SoD conflict** occurs when one person has two or more incompatible responsibilities that could allow them to misuse or bypass a process.

For example:

> **Creating AND approving the same payment.**

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. During an audit, you discover that the same employee can create a new vendor, enter the vendor's bank details, and approve payments to that vendor. What is the risk?

**Answer:**  
This is a **Segregation of Duties issue** because one employee has too much control over the payment process.

I would recommend separating the responsibilities so different authorized employees handle **vendor creation and payment approval**.
