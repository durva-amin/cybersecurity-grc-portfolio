# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is the Need-to-Know principle?

**Answer:**  
Need to Know means giving users access only to the specific information necessary to perform their job or task.

### Q2. Why is Need to Know important?

**Answer:**  
It limits unnecessary exposure of sensitive information and reduces the risk of unauthorized disclosure.

### Q3. Give an example of Need to Know.

**Answer:**  
Only authorized HR employees involved in a confidential investigation should have access to its documents.

### Q4. Does being an employee mean you should access all company information?

**Answer:**  
No. Employees should access only information necessary for their responsibilities.

### Q5. What is the difference between Least Privilege and Need to Know?

**Answer:**  
Least Privilege focuses on giving minimum required **permissions**, while Need to Know focuses on giving access only to necessary **information**.

### Q6. How does Need to Know protect confidential data?

**Answer:**  
It limits the number of people who can access sensitive information, reducing the chance of accidental or intentional disclosure.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A finance employee asks for access to confidential employee medical records, but those records are not required for their work. Should access be provided?

**Answer:**  
No.

According to the **Need-to-Know principle**, the employee should not receive access because the information is not necessary for their job.
