# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is Encryption?

**Answer:**  
Encryption is the process of converting readable plaintext into unreadable ciphertext using a cryptographic algorithm and key to protect information from unauthorized access.

### Q2. What is Plaintext?

**Answer:**  
Plaintext is the original readable information before encryption.

### Q3. What is Ciphertext?

**Answer:**  
Ciphertext is the unreadable encrypted form of the original information.

### Q4. What is Decryption?

**Answer:**  
Decryption converts encrypted ciphertext back into readable plaintext using the appropriate key.

### Q5. Which CIA Triad principle does encryption primarily support?

**Answer:**  
**Confidentiality.**

### Q6. What is the difference between data at rest and data in transit?

**Answer:**  
Data at rest is stored data, while data in transit is data moving between systems or across a network.

### Q7. Does encryption make data completely secure?

**Answer:**  
No. Encryption significantly protects confidentiality, but security also depends on factors such as key management, access controls, implementation, and endpoint security.

### Q8. Why is key management important?

**Answer:**  
Because if encryption keys are exposed, stolen, lost, or poorly protected, the security of the encrypted data can be affected.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company stores confidential customer information on employee laptops. Management is worried that someone could read the data if a laptop is stolen. What would you recommend?

**Answer:**  
I would recommend appropriate **disk/device encryption** for sensitive data stored on laptops, along with proper key management and access controls.

This protects **data at rest** and reduces the risk of unauthorized disclosure if a device is stolen.
