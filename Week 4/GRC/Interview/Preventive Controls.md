# Interview Questions

### Q1. What is a Preventive Control?

**Answer:**  
A preventive control is a security measure designed to prevent or reduce the likelihood of a security incident before it occurs.

### Q2. Give examples of Preventive Controls.

**Answer:**  
Firewalls, MFA, access controls, strong passwords, security awareness training, door locks, and least privilege.

### Q3. What is the difference between Preventive and Detective Controls?

**Answer:**  
Preventive controls try to **stop incidents**, while detective controls help **identify incidents or suspicious activities**.

### Q4. Is MFA a Preventive Control?

**Answer:**  
Yes. MFA helps prevent unauthorized account access.

### Q5. Is a firewall Preventive or Technical?

**Answer:**  
It can be **both**.

- **Technical** describes **how the control is implemented**.
- **Preventive** describes **what the control does**.

A firewall is generally a **technical control** that can perform a **preventive function** by blocking unauthorized traffic.

### Q6. Can a Physical Control also be Preventive?

**Answer:**  
Yes. For example, a locked server-room door is a **physical control** and performs a **preventive function**.

# Scenario-Based Question

### Q. A company wants to stop unauthorized employees from accessing confidential files. It configures access permissions so only authorized employees can open them. What type of control is this?

**Answer:**  
It is a **Preventive Control** because it prevents unauthorized access.

It is also implemented as a **Technical Control**.
